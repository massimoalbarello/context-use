# Trips conventions

This subtree inherits the [[agents|root guide]]. A trip is the occasion of being away: a
journey with a departure, one or more destinations and a return.

## Resolve a trip

Apply the root [[agents#identifiability-is-the-threshold|identifiability invariant]] to a
particular journey that evidence establishes happened and locates in time and destination. A
booking alone or an unresolved intention such as “going to Japan at some point” does not
establish which journey happened.

Displacement distinguishes a trip from an [[events/agents|event]]. A conference is the
occasion the owner attended; the journey that carried them there is a separate subject. Both
exist when the evidence resolves both, and neither is created merely to mirror the other.

## Path and account

    trips/<YYYY>/<MM>/<YYYY-MM-DD>_<trip-slug>/

Use the departure date. Name the slug for the destination or purpose, such as
`japan-two-weeks`, not for a carrier, booking or provider record.

`intro` establishes where the owner went, when and why, who travelled with them and what the
journey amounted to. The displacement itself belongs here: routing, where they stayed, how
the days were shaped, and the impressions of a destination that no single occasion owns.
Assets belong with the occasion they document; a photograph of one dinner belongs to that
event, not to the trip folder.

Create and link every identified person, organization and place under its own guide. Add a
further view only for an independently useful aspect, such as durable travel or access
knowledge worth reusing on a later journey.

## Trip timeline

Every trip begins with both `intro` and `timeline`. This is a local exception to the root
optional-timeline default because the sequence of the journey is the subject.

`timeline` is the spine of the trip: one dated line per contained occasion, linking that
event and saying what it was, alongside the dated particulars of the journey itself such as
a departure, a border crossing, a change of plan or a return. Keep each line at date
granularity and let the linked account carry the substance; a line that grows into a
paragraph belongs on the occasion's own page.

    - **11 August** — [[events/…/intro|Web Summit talk]] — gave the pricing talk; the room
      pushed back hard on usage billing.
    - **9 August** — flew Zurich to Lisbon; [[places/…/intro|Alfama]] for the week.

Do not retell contained events here, and do not write the trip as a day-by-day narrative.
The owner's day narrative is the [[about/diary/agents|diary]], composed separately.
