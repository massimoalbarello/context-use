# Topics conventions

Follow the [[agents|root guide]] for every convention not specific to topics.

`topics/` is for a subject the owner keeps returning to — an idea, field, discipline,
practice or piece of regulation — that other pages need to point at. It is not a tag
index, a glossary or a list of interests.

    topics/<topic-slug>/
    ├── intro     — the canonical account of the subject and the owner's position on it
    └── timeline  — material changes in the subject or in the owner's relation to it

Keep `topics/` flat: a topic folder holds pages, never another topic. `library/` holds the
works; `topics/` holds what the works are about.
