# Projects conventions

This subtree inherits the [[about/agents|About conventions]]. A project is an enduring
body of work the owner builds, operates or stewards whose identity survives individual
deliverables.

    about/projects/<slug>/
    ├── intro       — what the project is, why it exists and its durable boundaries
    └── timeline    — optional, curated milestones that materially changed it

Keep the project's purpose, boundaries, durable design, principles and outcomes. Link
related tasks and entities rather than absorbing their accounts.
