# About conventions

This subtree inherits the [[agents|root guide]]. It contains knowledge whose subject is the
owner.

## Owner entry point

Create `about/intro` on the first write that establishes who the owner is. Reconcile it as
that changes. It says who they are, what they do and the durable high-level context needed to
orient a reader, and it routes to independently useful owner-specific pages. Its content is
not managed by the default template.

The owner is the subject most likely to overflow `intro`. Move an independently retrievable
aspect—medical history, finances, schooling, family, personal history or an important
arrangement—to its own page in `about/`, and link it from the sentence that introduces it.
These are examples, not required categories.

Do not make `about/intro` a directory of people. Each person has a canonical page under
[[people/agents|People]] that owns their relationship with the owner. Link only people needed
to explain who the owner is.

## The owner's chronology

`about/` has no `timeline`. The owner participates in everything, so it would duplicate the
union of all entity timelines. The [[about/diary/agents|diary]] is the owner's timeline in
connected prose.

Activity belongs on the timeline of the most specific entity involved. Activity with no more
specific identifiable subject belongs to an applicable [[topics/agents|topic]]. The
[[automations/diary-composer/instructions|diary composer]] reads those dated changes later.

Never add to an existing `about/timeline`. As its lines are encountered, move them to the
entities involved and archive it once empty.

## Local subject boundaries

- The **diary** connects what happened on a day, what the owner expressed, and how work moved.
- A **project** is an enduring body of work that contains several finite pursuits.
- A **task** is a finite pursuit, experiment or consequential decision frame.
- A **topic** is an enduring subject in the world: a practice, field, lens or principle.
- Another page in **about** owns a durable particular whose subject is the owner themselves.

A health practice is a topic; the owner's diagnoses and test results are about the owner. An
investment thesis is a topic because it judges a subject in the world; the owner's mortgage
is their own particular. When one page mixes both kinds of subject, split it. When the subject
remains unclear, prefer the topic until evidence resolves something more specific.
