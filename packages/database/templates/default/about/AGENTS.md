# About conventions

This subtree inherits the [[agents|root guide]]. It adds only conventions for knowledge
whose subject is the owner.

Use `about/intro` as the concise, instance-owned overview of the owner and the most useful
routes into their knowledge.

The default template supplies three subdirectories:

- [[about/diary/agents|Diary]] — chronological lived context and current activity.
- [[about/projects/agents|Projects]] — enduring bodies of work the owner builds or stewards.
- [[about/tasks/agents|Tasks]] — finite outcomes, experiments and decision frames.

Other owner-subject knowledge might include health, finances, education, family, personal
history, preferences, beliefs, routines or goals. These are examples, not prescribed
categories.
