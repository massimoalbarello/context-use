# About conventions

Everything whose subject is the owner. Four areas, divided by time and by kind:

- **`chapters/`** — the owner's past, as lived. One folder per period of life.
- **`projects/`** — what they built. One folder per project, whatever period it spans.
- **`diary/`** — their present. One folder per day. ([[about/diary/agents|conventions]])
- **`tasks/`** — their substantial efforts and decision frames. One folder per effort. ([[about/tasks/agents|conventions]])

`about/intro` is the entry point, and anything working anywhere in this base reads it
first. Keep it short: who the owner is, what they are doing now, and links out. It is
not a biography — the chapters are.

## Chapters and projects

Both are period folders, named `<YYYY>_<slug>` or `<YYYY>-<YYYY>_<slug>` so the
directory sorts chronologically: `about/chapters/2020-2024_zurich/`,
`about/projects/2015_alarm-clock/`.

- **`story`** — the narrative. Required, and usually the only page.
- **`intro`** — used in place of `story` when the folder is an umbrella with pages
  beneath it. Then it is a short overview that links out, never the whole thing.
- Pictures and videos upload into the folder and embed from the page they illustrate.

A chapter is a **stretch of life**; a project is a **thing that was made**. They overlap
on purpose — the chapter narrates the years and links the projects, the project explains
the thing and links the chapter. Neither retells the other.

Split an umbrella into pages as soon as it has sections worth linking separately.

## What does not belong here

- **Judgement about a company or a person** — that is a lens page in their folder, per
  [[agents#where-a-page-belongs|the subject test]]. What stays here is
  the owner's process: the criteria they judge by, the intents they are pursuing.
- **Where anything stands** — the diary, always.

Chapters and projects are written in the past tense and stay written. When something new
comes to light, edit the page to say what is now true; the diary records that it did.

## Local rules

- **Write in the owner's voice, from what they have actually said.** These are the pages
  most likely to be published one day, and an invented motivation in a life story is
  worse than a gap. Mark inference as inference.
- **A chapter with no pictures and no links is a CV entry.** The value is the texture and
  the connections — the people, the places, the projects it produced.
