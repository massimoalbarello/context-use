# Tasks conventions

This subtree inherits the [[about/agents|About conventions]]. A task is a finite outcome,
experiment or consequential decision frame that can resolve or close and whose reasoning is
useful beyond a simple to-do.

## Identity and boundary

Apply the root [[agents#identifiability-is-the-threshold|identifiability invariant]] to the
pursuit or question. An indefinitely recurring practice is a [[topics/agents|topic]]; work
that decomposes into several finite pursuits is a [[about/projects/agents|project]].

An open question—whether to commit, hire, buy, ship, accept or walk away—becomes a task when
the evidence shows the owner weighing more than one defensible answer and establishes useful
decision context: constraints, alternatives, what has been ruled out, what would change the
answer or who must agree. A dated leaning without that frame stays on the subject it concerns.

The related organization, person and project keep their own accounts. This task owns the
finite question and the owner's reasoning about it.

## Unit of work

A task groups evidence for one pursuit across artifacts. Two pull requests continuing one
scope, a ticket reopened under another number, or a design followed by its implementation are
one task when that is the unit the owner would later retrieve. Link every artifact in the
sentence saying what it contributed and retain the particulars it established.

Do not create one task per provider artifact, but do not merge distinct outcomes merely
because they share a repository or project.

## Shape and lifecycle

    about/tasks/<slug>/

`intro` says what the pursuit is, why it matters, its durable constraints and alternatives,
and how the owner frames it. Add another view only for an independently useful aspect.

Timeline events include the task being framed, a consequential change in scope or direction,
a decision, handoff, abandonment, supersession or completion. Routine progress and transient
status do not qualify.

Resolution always receives a dated timeline event saying whether the task completed, was
decided, abandoned or superseded, with a link to the outcome when one exists. Keep the closed
task as its reasoning record and reconcile the resulting truth onto the durable subject that
owns it.
