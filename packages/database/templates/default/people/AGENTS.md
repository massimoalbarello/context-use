# People conventions

This subtree inherits the [[agents|root guide]]. It contains people as subjects.

## Resolve a person

Apply the root [[agents#identifiability-is-the-threshold|identifiability invariant]]. A
supported name plus company, role, profile, contact detail, stable source reference or a
specific interaction can distinguish someone from plausible namesakes. Context elsewhere in
the evidence counts: a given name may resolve through the organization or recurring duty
attached to it.

A lone display name, given name or handle stays plain text while genuine ambiguity remains.
If one display name refers to multiple people, keep separate pages and explain the distinction.

## Person account

`intro` explains who the person is and what they are to the owner. Include supported context
that will matter later: where they work, what they do there, how the owner came to know them,
what they are working on and what is useful before the next interaction. Omit unsupported
categories rather than filling a form.

Write about this person in prose and link their organization, introducer, projects and other
subjects in the sentences explaining those relationships. Most people need only `intro`,
commonly at `people/<first-last>/`.

Add another view when one aspect is independently worth retrieving or maintaining, such as
contact and location details, roles over time, or the owner's view of one relationship
context. Ground judgements in evidence and write with the care appropriate to a real person.

Timeline events include meaningful changes in the person's work, role, company, projects or
relationship with the owner. Do not leave a dated “currently” snapshot in `intro` when the
knowledge is a development. Lines here commonly link the organizations, occurrences and work
the development involved:

    - **3 March** — moved from engineering lead to CTO at [[companies/…/intro|Northwind]],
      taking [[about/projects/…/intro|the platform rebuild]] with her.
