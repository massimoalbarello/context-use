# Companies conventions

Follow the [[agents|root guide]] for every convention not specific to companies.

    companies/<company-slug>/
    ├── intro                 — what the company is and why it matters here
    ├── timeline              — material history between the owner and company
    ├── <fact-topic>          — an independently useful company fact
    └── as-<relationship>     — the owner's view in one relationship context

Most folders can remain a single `intro` page. Fact pages use a plain topic name such as
`history`, `products` or `leadership` and describe the company; lens pages use
`as-<relationship>` for a context-dependent view of what it is to the owner.

Engagement means the organization is one the owner is actually dealing with: they work in
or for it, it was the subject of a conversation they took part in, they are evaluating,
negotiating with, transacting with or partnering with it, they correspond with its people
about its business, or it is the affiliation of someone they engage with. One such
engagement earns the folder.

Name and link the people the owner deals with there, in the sentence saying what each of
them does. Only those people: the page is not a staff directory.
