# Events conventions

Follow the [[agents|root guide]] for every convention not specific to events.

    events/<YYYY>/<MM>/<YYYY-MM-DD>_<event-slug>/
    ├── intro        — what it was, where, why the owner went and what happened
    └── takeaways    — what changed or became useful because of it

An event is defined by an occasion with a time and place: a conference, trip, dinner,
hackathon, demo day or wedding. A [[meetings/agents|meeting]] is instead defined by the
conversation and participants. A multi-day event is one occurrence and usually one folder.
