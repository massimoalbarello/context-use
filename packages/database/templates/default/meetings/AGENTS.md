# Meetings conventions

Follow the [[agents|root guide]] for every convention not specific to meetings.

    meetings/<YYYY>/<MM>/<YYYY-MM-DD>_<meeting-slug>/
    ├── prep        — background and questions prepared beforehand
    ├── intro       — the useful account after the conversation happens
    └── transcript  — exceptional raw source when no durable source survives

A confirmed future meeting may begin with `prep` alone; this is a lifecycle exception to
the root `intro` entry-point convention. An [[events/agents|event]] is defined by its
occasion; a meeting is defined by its conversation.

A meeting folder is useful when forgetting the conversation would lose a meaningful
decision, insight, introduction, commitment or relationship milestone.

    # <What it was> — <D Month YYYY>               ← intro

    **With:** [[people/…|Name]] ([[companies/…|Company]]) or <plain name>
    **Where:** in person / call · **Why:** one line.

    ## What was said
    ## What I took away
    ## Commitments made

Distil the conversation rather than pasting a transcript, and keep what another
participant said distinct from what the owner concluded.
