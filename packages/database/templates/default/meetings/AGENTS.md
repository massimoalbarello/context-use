# Meetings conventions

This subtree inherits the [[agents|root guide]]. A meeting is one bounded conversation.

## Resolve a meeting

Apply the root [[agents#identifiability-is-the-threshold|identifiability invariant]] when
evidence resolves who participated, roughly when the conversation happened and what it was
about. A record, call or note can establish that it happened. An invitation without evidence
of the conversation cannot.

A recurring sync receives one folder per occurrence. A written exchange running over time is
a [[threads/agents|thread]]. Correspondence that only arranges a meeting belongs with that
meeting and usually needs no separate account.

An [[events/agents|event]] is defined by its occasion. When evidence independently resolves
both the occasion and a bounded conversation within it, create and link both accounts; do not
create either merely to mirror the other.

## Path and lifecycle

    meetings/<YYYY>/<MM>/<YYYY-MM-DD>_<meeting-slug>/

After the conversation, `intro` is the entry point. A confirmed future meeting may begin with
`prep` alone—a lifecycle exception to the root `intro` default. For a confirmed consequential
future meeting, create or reconcile `prep` after reading earlier occurrences, correspondence,
tasks and shared entities. Research only missing identity or role facts needed for the
conversation, then reconcile useful context, unknowns and the owner's intended questions
without claiming the meeting happened. Preserve `prep` in its pre-meeting form after the
meeting. If cancelled, retain or archive it according to whether its research remains useful.

Reserve `transcript` for a raw record whose source will not survive, mark it as raw, and still
distil an `intro`. Assets belong in the meeting folder and embed from the relevant page.

## Conversation account

`intro` establishes who participated, when and how they met, why, and what the conversation
produced: the substance in its figures, names, dates, conditions and reasons; what the owner
concluded; and what anyone agreed at that moment. Let the conversation determine headings and
length rather than creating empty categories.

Create and link every resolvable attendee, organization and subject named in the conversation.
Keep participant assertions distinct from the owner's conclusions.

Index the meeting on a person's or company's timeline when it is a material relationship
milestone for that entity. If the account relies materially on delayed memory, say so.

A meeting has no separate timeline because its dated occurrence page is already its account.
