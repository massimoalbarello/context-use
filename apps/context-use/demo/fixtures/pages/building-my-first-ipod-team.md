# Building my first iPod team

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) depend on [Jon Rubinstein](context-use://entity/jon-rubinstein) to bring the hardware work together and [Tony Fadell](context-use://entity/tony-fadell) to lead the first [iPod](context-use://entity/ipod) team. [Jony Ive](context-use://entity/jony-ive) and his group shape the industrial design; Jon later credits [Phil Schiller](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) with the wheel idea.

The [Rubinstein oral history](context-use://record/rubinstein-history) and [Fadell interview account](context-use://record/fadell-history) are later recollections of this work. I keep that distinction from contemporary notes; they do not establish a particular meeting on this page’s checkpoint date.
