# Building my first iPod team

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) need one experience to emerge from several disciplines. In the [team review](context-use://record/ipod-team-charter), [Jon](context-use://entity/jon-rubinstein) owns the hardware program and the seams between teams; [Tony](context-use://entity/tony-fadell) owns engineering execution; [Jony](context-use://entity/jony-ive) owns the object in the hand; [Phil](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) owns explaining why it matters.

The responsibility they share is the [library-to-listening journey](context-use://page/bringing-music-into-the-mac). A good enclosure cannot excuse difficult navigation, and a working player cannot excuse a broken sync. I asked Jon to keep failures at those boundaries in the same issue list.

The [empty-library rehearsal thread](context-use://record/ipod-product-review) turns that principle into tomorrow's test: let the audience see a collection arrive on the player, then find an album without explaining the wheel first. Tony owns the reset and I will attend the final rehearsal.
