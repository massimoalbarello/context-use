# Taking iTunes to the TV

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) am moving the [digital-hub journey](context-use://page/bringing-music-into-the-mac) into the living room. Apple TV is shipping at $299; the [launch thread](context-use://record/apple-tv-shipping) follows media from iTunes on a Mac or PC onto a television.

[Ron](context-use://entity/ron-johnson) caught the part a polished demo can hide: a customer has to get through the home-network setup. I asked him and [Phil](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) to start where the collection lives, just as we did in the [first iPod rehearsal](context-use://page/walking-the-first-ipod-demo-with-tony).

The [television partnership](context-use://page/working-with-bob-to-put-television-in-itunes) supplies content. It becomes a useful product only when purchase, setup and playback form a path a person can repeat at home.
