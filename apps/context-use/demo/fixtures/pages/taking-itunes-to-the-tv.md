# Taking iTunes to the TV

Our music and video library now has a place in the living room. [Apple](context-use://entity/apple) is shipping Apple TV, bringing [iTunes](context-use://entity/itunes) content from a [Mac](context-use://entity/mac) or PC to a widescreen television.

[Phil](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) explains the wireless playback in our [shipping announcement](context-use://record/apple-tv-shipping). [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) see another destination for the media customers already keep in their libraries, alongside [our portable players](context-use://page/taking-our-music-experience-beyond-the-mac).
