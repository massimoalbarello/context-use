# Responding to our early iPhone customers

After [our price reduction](context-use://page/lowering-the-price-of-our-iphone), [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) acknowledge that we need to care for the people who bought early. The [contemporaneous report of my letter](context-use://record/iphone-credit) describes a $100 Apple store credit, with details still to follow.

I keep that response beside the pricing decision. It records a correction in how we treat customers, not a reversal of the new price.
