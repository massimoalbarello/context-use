# Responding to our early iPhone customers

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) approved a $100 Apple store credit for eligible early iPhone customers. The [September 6 Gmail decision](context-use://record/iphone-credit) follows yesterday's [Slack escalation from Ron](context-use://record/iphone-price-reaction): people felt punished for trusting us early.

The [price cut](context-use://page/lowering-the-price-of-our-iphone) was meant to broaden access. Our explanation of holiday reach did not answer the concern created by the [original offer](context-use://page/introducing-the-phone-we-built). I should have brought the [earlier-purchase principle](context-use://page/completing-the-album) into the pricing review before customers had to remind us.

[Phil](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) owns the letter and explanation; [Ron](context-use://entity/ron-johnson) owns the store instructions, with [Tim](context-use://entity/tim-cook) checking execution. The credit is committed; eligibility and redemption details still need to follow. I have asked for an explicit early-buyer impact question in the next pricing review. A sales milestone will not close this trust obligation.
