# Helping people use what we make

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) learned something useful in the [2001 opening-weekend thread](context-use://record/stores-weekend): people liked the demonstration and still needed a next step for their own collection. That is why I keep [the store lesson](context-use://page/welcoming-our-first-store-customers) near the product work.

It shaped the [iPhone first-hour plan](context-use://page/preparing-the-first-customer-hour-with-ron): setup, a call, a sync, a web page, and a named person when the flow stops. The [June 30 debrief](context-use://record/iphone-first-weekend) tests the plan against what actually happened. [Ron](context-use://entity/ron-johnson) owns closing the loop with stores; [Scott](context-use://entity/scott-forstall) owns software cases.

September added another kind of help. The [credit decision](context-use://page/responding-to-our-early-iphone-customers) came from hearing the concern behind the question, not repeating the price-cut explanation. The [Plus pricing change](context-use://page/lowering-itunes-plus-prices) similarly follows questions that Ron carried across several reviews.

For the next launch, I want the product promise, the stopped task and the follow-up owner in the same conversation. The customer should not have to understand our organization to get an answer.
