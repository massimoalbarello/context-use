# Putting a display into the smaller nano

[Nano](context-use://entity/ipod-nano) gives us a very small flash player while keeping a color screen and the wheel. [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) want the difference from [shuffle](context-use://page/choosing-a-simpler-player-with-shuffle) to be a useful choice: choose an album on the device, or prepare a mix beforehand.

In the [launch-day Slack thread](context-use://record/nano-launch), [Jony](context-use://entity/jony-ive) makes size part of the demonstration and [Phil](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) owns the explanation. The 2GB/$199 and 4GB/$249 versions are available today.

The [ROKR release email](context-use://record/rokr-launch) describes the other product we introduced today. I asked [Tony](context-use://entity/tony-fadell) to compare the same music task on both tomorrow. Nano gives us a baseline for a product where the team can change the whole interaction.
