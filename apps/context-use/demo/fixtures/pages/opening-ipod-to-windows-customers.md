# Opening iPod to Windows customers

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) am widening [iPod](context-use://entity/ipod) beyond Mac owners. Our [new-model announcement](context-use://record/ipod-windows) includes [Windows](context-use://entity/windows) versions, with late-August availability planned.

Those customers use [Musicmatch Jukebox](context-use://entity/musicmatch-jukebox), while the Mac stays with [iTunes](context-use://entity/itunes). The higher-capacity models gain a touch-sensitive wheel. Windows-compatible iPod hardware and iTunes for Windows are separate steps.
