# Reaching one hundred million iPods

We have sold 100 million iPods across the family. [Tim's milestone thread](context-use://record/ipod-100m) distinguishes a cumulative result from a single model or year's sales.

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) am taking the result back to the [original library-to-pocket test](context-use://page/walking-the-first-ipod-demo-with-tony). The journey now reaches far more people than the first player did. That raises the cost of a poor handoff; it does not prove the [iPhone launch promise](context-use://page/introducing-the-phone-we-built) is ready.

[Tim](context-use://entity/tim-cook) owns the measured count and [Phil](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) its explanation. I asked for the early checklist in the phone review so the milestone creates a useful question about the next product, not just a celebration of the last one.
