# Introducing the phone we built

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) have made the [iPhone](context-use://entity/iphone) promise public: June US availability, 4GB at $499 and 8GB at $599, with Cingular as the exclusive carrier. The [post-Macworld email](context-use://record/iphone-unveiling) records the next owners as well as the announcement.

The [rehearsed transitions](context-use://page/walking-the-iphone-demonstration-with-scott) are the visible result of the [ROKR lesson](context-use://page/comparing-nano-and-rokr-with-tony). We chose to own the interaction across music, calling and the Internet. The [Macworld photograph](context-use://asset/steve-presenting-iphone) marks the moment the private work became a public commitment.

I assigned [Scott](context-use://entity/scott-forstall) software readiness, [Tim](context-use://entity/tim-cook) product and supply readiness, and [Ron](context-use://entity/ron-johnson) the retail handoff. Ron is building the first customer hour with Scott. The [2001 store lesson](context-use://page/welcoming-our-first-store-customers) applies again: watching me do it is not the same as being able to do it yourself.
