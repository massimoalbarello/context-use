# Introducing the phone we built

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) unveiled [iPhone](context-use://entity/iphone) as a phone, a widescreen iPod and an Internet device in one. Our [announcement](context-use://record/iphone-unveiling) commits to US availability in June: 4GB for $499 or 8GB for $599, with Cingular, later [AT&T](context-use://entity/at-t), as exclusive carrier.

Safari, Mail, maps and Visual Voicemail make [our touch-interface work](context-use://page/building-a-phone-around-our-fingers) tangible. [This photograph of my presentation](context-use://asset/steve-presenting-iphone) shows the public unveiling; the [previous rehearsal page](context-use://page/walking-the-iphone-demonstration-with-scott) is explicitly fictional.
