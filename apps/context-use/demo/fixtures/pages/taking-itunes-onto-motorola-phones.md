# Taking iTunes onto Motorola phones

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) agreed to explore music on phones with [Motorola](context-use://entity/motorola). The [partnership email](context-use://record/motorola-plan) sets a first-half-2005 availability plan and assigns [Tony](context-use://entity/tony-fadell) a more useful question: what happens when a song becomes a call and then becomes music again?

This revisits the compromise from [Musicmatch](context-use://page/opening-ipod-to-windows-customers). A partner can extend our reach, but the customer still experiences the seam as part of the product. [Owning Windows iTunes](context-use://page/bringing-our-own-itunes-to-windows) showed what we gained by closing one such seam.

I want Tony to write down the responsibilities across our software, the handset and the carrier, then bring back a real sequence to review. [Phil](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) owns keeping the promise accurate: this is a Motorola phone with iTunes, not an Apple phone.
