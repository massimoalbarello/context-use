# Taking iTunes onto Motorola phones

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) have announced a partnership with [Motorola](context-use://entity/motorola) to transfer music from Mac or PC iTunes to its handsets. Our [joint announcement](context-use://record/motorola-plan) expects the mobile player in the first half of 2005.

This is a partner’s handset carrying our music software. The agreement itself does not announce an Apple-designed phone.
