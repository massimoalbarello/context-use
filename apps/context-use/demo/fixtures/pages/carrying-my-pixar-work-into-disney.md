# Carrying my Pixar work into Disney

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) am trying to protect what makes [Pixar](context-use://entity/pixar) work while changing the structure around it. [Today's acquisition decision](context-use://page/choosing-disney-for-pixars-next-chapter) grows out of my [working relationship with Bob](context-use://page/working-with-bob-to-put-television-in-itunes), but it has a different test: can the creative culture survive the new ownership?

The [decision meeting](context-use://record/pixar-call) keeps [Ed's](context-use://entity/ed-catmull) and [John's](context-use://entity/john-lasseter) concerns beside the alternatives. The [follow-up email](context-use://record/pixar-announcement) makes my current responsibility clear. I still lead Pixar until the deal closes; the board role is conditional. I want that distinction available whenever someone asks me to act in the proposed role before it exists.
