# Changing the processor underneath our Mac

At WWDC [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) announced our move to [Intel](context-use://entity/intel) processors for the [Mac](context-use://entity/mac). Our [transition announcement](context-use://record/intel-plan) targets the first models within a year and the whole range by the end of 2007.

Developers get a transition kit to prepare applications for both architectures. This is another commitment I am carrying alongside [our music business](context-use://page/making-our-store-part-of-the-music-experience); it concerns Macs, not the phone’s processor.
