# Changing the processor underneath our Mac

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) committed to the first [Intel](context-use://entity/intel) Macs within a year and a full transition by the end of 2007. The [transition review](context-use://record/intel-plan) keeps the customer consequence beside the hardware schedule: people need their applications and work to remain useful.

This is the same responsibility I took on with [iTunes for Windows](context-use://page/bringing-our-own-itunes-to-windows). Changing the underlying path is only valuable if the person can continue their task. The Developer Transition Kit gives application teams a way to begin preparing.

[Tim](context-use://entity/tim-cook) owns the hardware sequence, [Scott](context-use://entity/scott-forstall) brings software-readiness risks, and [Phil](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) explains the overlap. I need this visible beside the [phone exploration](context-use://page/bringing-our-music-work-into-phones) so I do not promise the same people's attention to every project.
