# Preparing the first customer hour with Ron

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) asked [Ron](context-use://entity/ron-johnson) to name who helps when setup stops. His [June 27 Gmail thread](context-use://record/retail-handoff) identifies the weak point: a customer with an activation or account question needs a route between Apple and [AT&T](context-use://entity/at-t), not another feature demonstration.

This carries the [2001 store lesson](context-use://page/welcoming-our-first-store-customers) into a more consequential situation. A person may be setting up their only phone. The [readiness review](context-use://page/improving-iphone-before-customers-get-it) set the product configuration; now the human handoff has to match it.

[Scott](context-use://entity/scott-forstall) will join the walkthrough and distinguish device/software issues from carrier account issues. Ron owns keeping a person responsible until the customer knows the next step. We will review real customer reports after launch, not stop at a successful rehearsal.
