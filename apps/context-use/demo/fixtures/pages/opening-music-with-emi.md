# Opening music with EMI

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) am announcing a new choice with [EMI](context-use://entity/emi): higher-quality music without DRM on [iTunes](context-use://entity/itunes). Our [announcement](context-use://record/emi-drm-announcement) sets availability for May at $1.29 per song.

The existing 99-cent protected tracks remain. This is a commitment to open part of the catalog, with the actual launch still ahead.
