# Opening music with EMI

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) agreed to offer [EMI's](context-use://entity/emi) DRM-free music in May, at $1.29 per 256kbps AAC song, alongside the existing protected 99-cent option. The [announcement email](context-use://record/emi-drm-announcement) separates that future availability from a product someone can buy today.

The [original store model](context-use://page/selling-songs-one-at-a-time) made purchase convenient but carried restrictions. This agreement can remove one obstacle to using purchased music. The [name settlement](context-use://page/settling-our-apple-name) was a different question; it did not deliver these rights.

I asked [Phil](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) to describe what the customer can do and [Ron](context-use://entity/ron-johnson) to bring questions about eligibility and the two prices to May's review. We must test playback beyond our own devices. The whole catalog is not becoming DRM-free in this agreement.
