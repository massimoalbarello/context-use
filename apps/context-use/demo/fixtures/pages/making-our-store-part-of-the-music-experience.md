# Making our store part of the music experience

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) want buying a song to be an ordinary part of enjoying music. The [99-cent store decision](context-use://page/selling-songs-one-at-a-time) extends our existing library-to-pocket path instead of creating another collection to manage.

The [review](context-use://record/store-launch) assigns the parts: [Phil](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) explains the purchase and its restrictions; [Tony](context-use://entity/tony-fadell) proves playback on the device; I own the label relationships and the Windows decision. The store is Mac-only today. Until we address the [Musicmatch compromise](context-use://page/opening-ipod-to-windows-customers), the whole experience does not reach the same people as iPod.
