# Lowering iTunes Plus prices

We are lowering every [iTunes](context-use://entity/itunes) Plus song to 99 cents and expanding the selection beyond two million tracks. Our [announcement](context-use://record/itunes-plus-expansion) brings independent labels alongside [EMI](context-use://entity/emi) in the catalog without DRM.

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) can now connect [the May launch](context-use://page/opening-emi-purchases-beyond-drm) to a broader, less expensive offering. The 256kbps AAC format stays; the price and selection change.
