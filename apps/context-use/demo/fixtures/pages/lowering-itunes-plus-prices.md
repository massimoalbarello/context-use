# Lowering iTunes Plus prices

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) removed the iTunes Plus price fork: all Plus songs are now 99 cents, and the DRM-free catalog includes more than two million tracks from EMI and independent labels. The [October 17 Slack thread](context-use://record/itunes-plus-expansion) supersedes the $1.29 price from [May's launch](context-use://page/opening-emi-purchases-beyond-drm).

[Ron's](context-use://entity/ron-johnson) questions from May, carried into the [July milestone review](context-use://page/passing-three-billion-songs), explain why this is useful. Removing a restriction should not require a confusing purchase decision. The [Complete My Album principle](context-use://page/completing-the-album) still applies: make the next step feel fair and simple.

[Phil](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) owns the current offer and Ron the store explanation. I am preserving the old price in the decision history while removing it from the current recommendation. The whole store has not become DRM-free; the catalog boundary remains.
