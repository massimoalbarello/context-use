# Building a phone around our fingers

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) want to own the whole phone interaction, a direction that came out of the [ROKR debrief](context-use://page/comparing-nano-and-rokr-with-tony). The [January 7 review](context-use://record/touch-interface-review) brings that earlier evidence into a concrete choice: use fingers and software-defined controls, without a fixed keyboard or stylus.

[Scott](context-use://entity/scott-forstall) demonstrates music, a call, typing and browsing on one device. [Jony](context-use://entity/jony-ive) must judge the enclosure and the interaction together. The old [iPod team rule](context-use://page/building-my-first-ipod-team) still applies: each discipline can succeed while the customer's sequence fails.

I asked Scott to prepare a repeatable state for tomorrow's rehearsal and [Tony](context-use://entity/tony-fadell) to check music resuming after a call. This review does not settle retail setup, delivery readiness or third-party applications. Those remain work I have to assign and revisit.
