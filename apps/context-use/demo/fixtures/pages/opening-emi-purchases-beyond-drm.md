# Opening EMI purchases beyond DRM

The [May promise to offer EMI without DRM](context-use://page/opening-music-with-emi) is now delivered as [iTunes Plus](context-use://entity/itunes-store). The [launch Slack thread](context-use://record/itunes-plus) keeps the terms precise: $1.29, 256kbps AAC, with the 99-cent protected option still available.

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) asked [Ron](context-use://entity/ron-johnson) to test what that change means outside the usual Apple playback path. Removing a restriction is useful, but two versions of a song can introduce new confusion. [Phil](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) owns explaining the difference.

Ron is collecting price and eligibility questions for the next review. I want them beside the [Complete My Album principle](context-use://page/completing-the-album): make the next purchase feel like a fair continuation of the last one.
