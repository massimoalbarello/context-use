# Bringing our music work into phones

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) am testing whether our music experience can live inside a phone. The [Motorola partnership](context-use://page/taking-itunes-onto-motorola-phones) gives us a route, but it leaves control divided across software, handset and carrier.

My earlier [Windows bridge decision](context-use://page/opening-ipod-to-windows-customers) is the relevant precedent: reach may justify a temporary compromise, while [owning more of the journey](context-use://page/bringing-our-own-itunes-to-windows) may later remove it. The [email to Tony and Phil](context-use://record/motorola-plan) commits us to reviewing music, a call and the return to music together. We have a plan for the first half of 2005, not a completed phone experience.
