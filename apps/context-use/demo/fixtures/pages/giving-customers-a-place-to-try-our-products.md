# Giving customers a place to try our products

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) am extending the [iTunes lesson](context-use://page/bringing-music-into-the-mac) into a physical place. A good demonstration is only the beginning: a visitor needs to know what to do with their own music when they get home.

In the [retail review](context-use://record/stores-plan), [Ron](context-use://entity/ron-johnson) and I agreed to proceed with the first two stores and the plan for 25 this year. [Phil](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) will use the library journey at the music station. Ron will bring the actual questions from opening weekend to our May 21 review.

My open question is whether useful help survives a crowded store. I want visitors to take over the task themselves, rather than watch an employee perform it.
