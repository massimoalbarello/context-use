# Choosing a simpler player with shuffle

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) removed the screen from [shuffle](context-use://entity/ipod-shuffle), but the job of choosing music did not disappear. It moved into [iTunes AutoFill](context-use://entity/itunes). The [review](context-use://record/shuffle-launch) asks whether that tradeoff makes a useful small player rather than merely a device with fewer parts.

[Mini](context-use://page/making-ipod-smaller-with-mini) serves someone who wants to choose an album while carrying it. Shuffle serves a different listening habit. [Tony](context-use://entity/tony-fadell) must test AutoFill with an ordinary mixed library, and [Phil](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) must demonstrate preparation before playback.

I want to keep this decision beside the [original library principle](context-use://page/bringing-music-into-the-mac). Simplicity belongs to the whole workflow. The announced choices are 512MB at $99 and 1GB at $149.
