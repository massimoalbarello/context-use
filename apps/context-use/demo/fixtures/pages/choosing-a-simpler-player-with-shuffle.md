# Choosing a simpler player with shuffle

With [iPod shuffle](context-use://entity/ipod-shuffle), we are offering a USB flash player at $99 for 512MB or $149 for 1GB. Our [release](context-use://record/shuffle-launch) combines a playback-order switch with iTunes AutoFill.

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) can offer a different tradeoff from [mini’s display and wheel](context-use://page/making-ipod-smaller-with-mini): this player removes the screen and lets the library do more of the selection work.
