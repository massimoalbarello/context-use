# Joining Disney’s board when the deal closes

Disney has [completed the Pixar acquisition](context-use://record/pixar-close), and [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) have joined its board. [Ed Catmull](context-use://entity/ed-catmull) will lead the Pixar and Disney animation studios; [John Lasseter](context-use://entity/john-lasseter) becomes their chief creative officer.

I am now a Disney director, following my Pixar chairman and CEO role. The closing resolves the pending transaction in [our January agreement](context-use://page/choosing-disney-for-pixars-next-chapter).
