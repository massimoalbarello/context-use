# Joining Disney’s board when the deal closes

The acquisition has closed. The [May 5 email with Bob and Ed](context-use://record/pixar-close) changes my responsibility: [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) join [Disney's](context-use://entity/disney) board, while [Ed](context-use://entity/ed-catmull) leads the animation studios and [John](context-use://entity/john-lasseter) becomes chief creative officer.

I made the [January choice](context-use://page/choosing-disney-for-pixars-next-chapter) to protect a way of making films, not to send every operating decision through me. I asked Ed to escalate situations where the new structure undermines the creative agreement. [Bob](context-use://entity/bob-iger) and I need the difference between a board responsibility and running the studio to hold.

This matters to my wider life at work: the [Apple leadership handoff](context-use://page/recognizing-tim-and-planning-the-ipod-handoff) and [phone exploration](context-use://page/bringing-our-music-work-into-phones) are also changing what needs my attention. I should be clear about where I decide, where I advise, and who owns the next action.
