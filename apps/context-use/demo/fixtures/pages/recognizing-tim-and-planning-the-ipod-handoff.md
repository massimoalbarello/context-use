# Recognizing Tim and planning the iPod handoff

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) am changing my leadership structure while the [Intel transition](context-use://page/changing-the-processor-underneath-our-mac), [phone exploration](context-use://page/bringing-our-music-work-into-phones) and expanding iPod line compete for attention. The [handoff review](context-use://record/leadership) makes the capacity problem explicit.

[Tim](context-use://entity/tim-cook) becomes COO while continuing to lead the Macintosh Division. [Jon](context-use://entity/jon-rubinstein) plans to retire on March 31, 2006; [Tony](context-use://entity/tony-fadell) will succeed him in iPod engineering and report to me.

The [original team agreement](context-use://page/building-my-first-ipod-team) taught me to name owners at the seams. Jon and Tony will walk open decisions and supplier dependencies together. Tim brings allocation conflicts to the operating review. I want disputes about responsibility surfaced before they turn into missed dates.
