# Refreshing our MacBook

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) need to keep the [Mac](context-use://entity/mac) visible while iPhone takes so much attention. The [MacBook refresh thread](context-use://record/macbook-update) connects faster Intel processors, memory and storage to the libraries people continue to keep on their computers.

This is a continuation of the [Intel transition](context-use://page/changing-the-processor-underneath-our-mac) and the [digital-hub idea](context-use://page/bringing-music-into-the-mac). The new phone does not remove the computer's job of importing, organizing and moving a collection.

[Tim](context-use://entity/tim-cook) brings staffing and supply conflicts into one operating review. [Phil](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) and [Ron](context-use://entity/ron-johnson) will use real collections in the demonstrations. I must not let excitement about one project become an implicit decision to neglect another.
