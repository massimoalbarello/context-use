# Putting the first iPod in my pocket

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) introduced [iPod](context-use://entity/ipod) as 1,000 songs in your pocket. The [post-introduction email](context-use://record/ipod-launch) preserves both what we announced—5GB, $399, Mac-only—and what we still owe: availability on November 10.

The result grows out of the [iTunes library](context-use://page/bringing-music-into-the-mac) and the [rehearsal with Tony](context-use://page/walking-the-first-ipod-demo-with-tony). We can now show the whole path from a collection to listening away from the computer. The [original player](context-use://asset/original-ipod-photo) is a reminder of the physical constraint: it has to belong in a pocket.

I asked [Tony](context-use://entity/tony-fadell) and [Jon](context-use://entity/jon-rubinstein) to review the retail box, instructions and a real library together before shipment, with [Ron](context-use://entity/ron-johnson) involved in the setup handoff. The successful presentation does not close that work.
