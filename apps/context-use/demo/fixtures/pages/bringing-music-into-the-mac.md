# Bringing music into the Mac

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) want the [Mac](context-use://entity/mac) to take responsibility for a person's music collection. Today's [iTunes email thread with Phil and Jon](context-use://record/itunes-launch) gives us a test: bring in a CD, find a song, make a mix. The person using it should not need to understand our file organization first.

This is the starting point for my digital-hub work at [Apple](context-use://entity/apple). [Phil](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) owns explaining the sequence; [Jon](context-use://entity/jon-rubinstein) will use it to evaluate a portable player. I have asked him to avoid creating a second library that the customer must maintain. The useful question for the next review is whether the collection follows the person with less effort.
