# Completing the album

Buying one song should leave a sensible path to the rest of the album. [iTunes](context-use://entity/itunes) now credits previously purchased tracks toward qualifying albums, as our [announcement](context-use://record/complete-my-album) describes.

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) connect this to [selling songs individually](context-use://page/selling-songs-one-at-a-time): customers can try a single, then complete the album without paying for that track again. [Apple](context-use://entity/apple) initially gives them 180 days from their first song purchase.
