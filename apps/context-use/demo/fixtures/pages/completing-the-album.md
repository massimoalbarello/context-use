# Completing the album

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) want someone who tries a song and later buys the album to feel that the first purchase counted. [The Complete My Album thread](context-use://record/complete-my-album) makes that concrete: eligible prior tracks receive a 99-cent credit toward the album, initially within 180 days of the first song purchase.

This continues the [choice to sell songs individually](context-use://page/selling-songs-one-at-a-time). We should not penalize a customer for starting small. [Ron](context-use://entity/ron-johnson) will demonstrate the flow with an existing purchase and explain the eligibility window.

I am keeping the principle beyond this release: when we change an offer, inspect what happens to the person who already trusted the earlier offer. [Phil](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) owns making that continuation understandable.
