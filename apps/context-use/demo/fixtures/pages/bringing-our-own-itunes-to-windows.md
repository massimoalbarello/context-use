# Bringing our own iTunes to Windows

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) can now give [Windows](context-use://entity/windows) customers our own [iTunes](context-use://entity/itunes) library, Music Store and iPod synchronization. The [release-day email](context-use://record/itunes-windows) closes the experience gap from the [Musicmatch bridge](context-use://page/opening-ipod-to-windows-customers).

The bridge let us reach PC owners sooner. Owning the software now lets us take responsibility for the whole journey. That is why the [rehearsal with Phil](context-use://page/rehearsing-our-windows-message-with-phil) starts with buying a song and ends with listening on the player.

[Tony](context-use://entity/tony-fadell) owns checking the transition for existing collections. I asked him to involve [Ron](context-use://entity/ron-johnson): shipping an application is not evidence that a customer can find their music after moving to it.
