# My work from iPod to iPhone

It is October 17, 2007. [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) am deciding how to open the iPhone to developers while still carrying a Mac delivery date, an early-customer trust obligation and a different role at Disney. This is the context I want in front of me before the next conversation.

## What needs my attention

[I have promised a native SDK for February](context-use://page/committing-to-native-applications-for-iphone). Scott's July feedback changed the case for our original web-only approach. [Leopard is due October 26](context-use://page/setting-leopards-date). The [early-iPhone credit](context-use://page/responding-to-our-early-iphone-customers) still needs follow-through in these notes. [My commitment list](context-use://page/keeping-track-of-what-i-have-promised) keeps those owners and states separate.

## How I got here

1. [Make music travel with the person.](context-use://page/taking-our-music-experience-beyond-the-mac) iTunes and iPod began as one library-to-pocket experience. Musicmatch let us reach Windows users; our own Windows software later closed the gap.
2. [Learn where the seams hurt.](context-use://page/bringing-our-music-work-into-phones) ROKR made the limits of divided control tangible. That debrief led into the touch-interface decision, the Macworld rehearsal and the first customer hour. Owning the phone now gives me responsibility for opening it well.
3. [Make the next purchase feel simple and fair.](context-use://page/making-our-store-part-of-the-music-experience) Songs, television, Complete My Album and iTunes Plus changed what people could acquire and use. Customer questions carried across reviews explain today's Plus pricing change—and the question I missed before the iPhone price cut.
4. [Protect the work as my role changes.](context-use://page/carrying-my-pixar-work-into-disney) The television partnership built trust with Bob. The Pixar decision tested that trust against Ed and John's creative concerns. Closing moved me from running the studio to a board responsibility.

## What I want to remember

The [whole task](context-use://page/how-i-connect-hardware-and-software) is my unit of judgment. The [people and their responsibilities](context-use://page/the-people-i-build-with) let me act on it. The [customer evidence](context-use://page/helping-people-use-what-we-make) tells me when my explanation or design needs to change. I keep [rehearsal notes](context-use://page/my-working-notes-and-rehearsals), [partner boundaries](context-use://page/the-boundaries-i-work-across) and [visual references](context-use://page/pictures-of-me-and-our-pocket-devices) nearby to recover the detail before a conversation.

For one decision with its full trail, return to [the early-buyer credit](context-use://page/responding-to-our-early-iphone-customers): a pricing meeting, a retail Slack escalation and a Gmail decision explain why I changed the response. I want the reason available when the same kind of choice comes around again.
