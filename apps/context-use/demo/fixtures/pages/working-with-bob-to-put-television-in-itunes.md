# Working with Bob to put television in iTunes

[Bob](context-use://entity/bob-iger) and [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) have a concrete result to build trust around: US episodes of Lost and Desperate Housewives at $1.99, available after broadcast. Our [email exchange](context-use://record/itunes-video) keeps that window precise, alongside Pixar shorts and music videos in the store.

The [song-purchase model](context-use://page/selling-songs-one-at-a-time) gives us the starting point. [Video iPod](context-use://page/giving-our-ipod-a-video-screen) gives us a place for the result to travel. [Phil](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) owns showing the full purchase-and-play sequence and returning customer questions to both teams.

I am interested in how Bob works as well as what we can distribute. A modest promise delivered clearly tells me more about a partner than a large announcement. I want to remember that when our next conversation reaches beyond television.
