# Apple announces DRM-free EMI music for May

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

Apple announces that EMI’s digital catalog will be offered without DRM on iTunes in May, at $1.29 per 256kbps AAC song. Existing 99-cent protected tracks remain available. This is an announcement of future availability.

Source: [Apple announces DRM-free EMI music for May](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2007/04/02Apple-Unveils-Higher-Quality-DRM-Free-Music-on-the-iTunes-Store/).
