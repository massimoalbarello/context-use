# Disney agrees to acquire Pixar

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

Disney announces an all-stock acquisition of Pixar, subject to approvals. The agreement proposes a Disney board role for Jobs and animation leadership roles for Catmull and Lasseter.

Source: [Disney agrees to acquire Pixar](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/press-releases/disney-to-acquire-pixar/).
