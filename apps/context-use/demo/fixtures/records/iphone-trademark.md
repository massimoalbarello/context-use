# Cisco and Apple agree on the iPhone trademark

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

Apple and Cisco resolve their iPhone trademark dispute. Both companies may use the name worldwide, dismiss pending trademark actions and explore interoperability; other terms remain confidential.
