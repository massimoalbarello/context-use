# Fadell on joining Apple in 2001

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

CHM’s account of its May 10, 2017 conversation with Tony Fadell describes his recruitment as an Apple contractor in 2001 and his contribution to the first iPod. The interview took place much later than the work being recalled.

Source: [Fadell on joining Apple in 2001](https://computerhistory.org/blog/computing-for-the-whole-world-a-conversation-with-ipod-iphone-inventor-tony-fadell/).
