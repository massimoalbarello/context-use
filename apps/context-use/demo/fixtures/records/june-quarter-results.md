# Apple reports its June quarter and an iPhone sales goal

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

Apple reports record quarterly Mac shipments of 1,764,000 and sales of 9,815,000 iPods for the quarter ended June 30. Steve Jobs states a goal of selling the millionth iPhone by the end of its first full quarter of sales.

Source: [Apple reports its June quarter and an iPhone sales goal](https://www.apple.com/uk/newsroom/2007/07/25Apple-Reports-Third-Quarter-Results/).
