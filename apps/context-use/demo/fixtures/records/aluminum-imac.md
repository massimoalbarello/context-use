# iMac review: the computer should recede behind the work

Fictional demo · Synced from Granola · 2007-08-07

**Attendees:** Steve Jobs, Jony Ive, Phil Schiller, Tim Cook\
**Time:** 09:00–09:45 UTC

## Product
The new 20-inch and 24-inch iMacs use aluminum and glass with a thinner aluminum keyboard. They are available today and include iLife ’08.

## Review
Jony presents the enclosure and display as the setting for the customer’s work. Steve asks Phil to follow the hardware reveal with making something in iLife. The physical and software stories should connect, as they did with iPod and now iPhone.

## Follow-up
Phil and Ron will show a real photo collection becoming something a customer can share. Tim keeps Mac delivery visible in the operating review while the portable-product teams prepare September launches.
