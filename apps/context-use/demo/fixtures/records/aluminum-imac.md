# Apple unveils the aluminum and glass iMac

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

Apple introduces 20-inch and 24-inch iMacs with aluminum and glass enclosures, Intel Core 2 Duo processors and a thinner aluminum keyboard. The computers include iLife ’08 and are available immediately.

Source: [Apple unveils the aluminum and glass iMac](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2007/08/07Apple-Unveils-New-iMac/).
