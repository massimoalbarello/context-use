# Disney television and Pixar shorts arrive in iTunes

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

iTunes 6 offers music videos, Pixar shorts and US television downloads. Disney’s Lost and Desperate Housewives episodes cost $1.99, with new episodes offered after broadcast. Bob Iger publicly endorses the partnership.

Source: [Disney television and Pixar shorts arrive in iTunes](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2005/10/12Apple-Announces-iTunes-6-With-2-000-Music-Videos-Pixar-Short-Films-Hit-TV-Shows/).
