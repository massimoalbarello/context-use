Fictional demo · Synced from Granola · 2007-01-08

**Attendees:** Steve Jobs, Scott Forstall, Phil Schiller, Tony Fadell\
**Time:** 09:00–09:45 UTC

## Sequence
Steve introduces a widescreen iPod, a phone and an Internet device, then reveals that they are one product. The run moves from music to a call to the browser. Scott slows the keyboard and page-zoom sections so the interaction can be understood.

## Decisions
Keep the transitions visible. A jump between prepared screens would hide the very thing the ROKR review taught us to test. Phil removes a secondary feature explanation that interrupts the sequence.
