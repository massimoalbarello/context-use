# iPhone announcement and US launch plan

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

Jobs introduces a multi-touch phone, widescreen iPod and Internet device. The US plan is June availability: 4GB/$499 and 8GB/$599 through exclusive carrier Cingular. Safari, Mail, Google Maps and Visual Voicemail are announced.

Source: [iPhone announcement and US launch plan](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2007/01/09Apple-Reinvents-the-Phone-with-iPhone/).
