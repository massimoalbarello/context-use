# Music Store review: make buying easier than managing files

Fictional demo · Synced from Granola · 2003-04-28

**Attendees:** Steve Jobs, Phil Schiller, Tony Fadell\
**Time:** 09:00–09:45 UTC

## Decision
Launch the Mac store with individual songs at 99 cents and no subscription requirement. A customer should find a song, buy it and have it available in the library that already feeds their iPod.

## Why this matters
The original iPod solved carrying a collection; it did not solve acquiring music. Steve wants the purchase and sync steps judged together. Phil will show a purchase becoming a playable song rather than ending the demo at the receipt.

## Constraints and owners
- Phil: explain the purchase model and the rights restrictions plainly.
- Tony: verify purchased tracks reach the player through the normal sync flow.
- Steve: carry the label relationships and the decision to extend the store to Windows.
- Open: the PC customer still depends on the Musicmatch bridge. The store increases the cost of leaving that gap open.
