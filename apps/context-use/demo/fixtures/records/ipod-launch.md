# Original iPod announcement and planned availability

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

The original iPod offered a 5 GB drive, a mechanical scroll wheel, FireWire transfer and charging, and iTunes 2 Auto-Sync. Apple advertised up to 1,000 songs and ten hours of playback. It announced $399 US pricing and November 10 availability.

Source: [Original iPod announcement and planned availability](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2001/10/23Apple-Presents-iPod/).
