### Scott Forstall · 16:00 UTC

Following the June 11 review, three recurring requests need a better answer: useful operation offline, access to device capabilities, and performance for richer interfaces. A bookmark to a website is not enough for some of the proposed applications.

### Phil Schiller · 16:12 UTC

Developers also need to know whether we are collecting feedback or committing to a native distribution model. I do not want the follow-up to imply a release date we have not agreed.

### Steve Jobs · 16:24 UTC

Keep the use cases and work through a native approach with the protection model beside it. The launch choice bought us a dependable starting point; it should not become an argument for ignoring what people want to build. Bring a scoped proposal before we commit publicly.
