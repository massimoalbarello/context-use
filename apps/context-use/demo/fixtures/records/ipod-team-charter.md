# iPod team review: one owner for every handoff

Fictional demo · Synced from Granola · 2001-10-19

**Attendees:** Steve Jobs, Jon Rubinstein, Tony Fadell, Jony Ive, Phil Schiller\
**Time:** 09:00–09:45 UTC

## Working agreement
Jon owns the hardware program and the interfaces between teams. Tony owns getting the player through engineering and into the demo. Jony owns the object in the hand: size, controls and feel. Phil owns explaining the benefit without a technical preamble.

## Tension
A smaller enclosure is appealing, but Steve will not trade away the ability to find an album quickly. The wheel, display and software have to be judged together. A successful component review does not prove that the whole sequence works.

## Decisions and actions
- Tony: run the library-to-player-to-album sequence with Jony before Monday’s rehearsal.
- Jon: keep synchronization failures in the same issue list as device failures.
- Phil: open with the pocket, then demonstrate the music. Avoid a tour of specifications.
- Steve: attend the final rehearsal on October 22.
