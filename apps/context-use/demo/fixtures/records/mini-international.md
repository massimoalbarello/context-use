# Revised international iPod mini availability

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

Apple says the already available US mini is scheduled for international availability on July 24. This supersedes the April international expectation in January’s announcement.

Source: [Revised international iPod mini availability](https://www.apple.com/uk/newsroom/2004/07/07Apple-iPod-mini-International-Availability-Set-for-July-24/).
