**Attendees:** Steve Jobs, Bob Iger, Ed Catmull, John Lasseter\

## Alternatives
Continue Pixar with a distribution agreement, or join Disney with aligned ownership. Ed warned that a transaction can look successful while quietly damaging the process that produces the films. John wants creative reviews to stay candid and centered on the story.

## Steve’s decision criterion
The iTunes television work showed Bob could turn a conversation into a concrete shared result. That builds confidence, but it is not sufficient. The Pixar decision must protect the people and practices that make good films possible.

## Agreement for the next chapter
Proceed with the acquisition proposal, subject to approvals. Ed and John will carry animation leadership; Steve will take a board role when the deal closes. Bob and Ed will bring organizational decisions back to the question of creative quality, not simply integration speed.

## Open
Signing and closing are different. Keep the current responsibilities in place until the transaction completes.
