# Apple introduces iLife ’08

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

iLife ’08 introduces iPhoto Events and a rebuilt iMovie, with photo and video sharing through .Mac Web Gallery. The suite also updates iWeb and GarageBand.
