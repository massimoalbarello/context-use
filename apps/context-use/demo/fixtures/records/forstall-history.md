# Forstall and the original iPhone engineers look back

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

CHM’s account of its June 20, 2017 interviews describes Forstall’s software leadership, Jobs’s preference for fingers over a stylus, and secret recruitment into the phone team. The account does not establish an exact date for the initial touch decision.

Source: [Forstall and the original iPhone engineers look back](https://computerhistory.org/blog/creating-magic-a-conversation-with-original-iphone-engineers-software-team-lead-scott-forstall/).
