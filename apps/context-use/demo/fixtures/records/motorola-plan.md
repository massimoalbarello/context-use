# Motorola and Apple announce mobile iTunes partnership

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

The partners plan a mobile iTunes player on Motorola handsets, transferring music from a Mac or PC. Their stated availability window is the first half of 2005.

Source: [Motorola and Apple announce mobile iTunes partnership](https://www.apple.com/uk/newsroom/2004/07/26Motorola-and-Apple-Bring-iTunes-Music-Player-to-Motorolas-Next-Generation-Mobile-Phones/).
