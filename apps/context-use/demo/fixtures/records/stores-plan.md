# Retail review: sell the first successful experience

Fictional demo · Synced from Granola · 2001-05-15

**Attendees:** Steve Jobs, Ron Johnson, Phil Schiller\
**Time:** 09:00–09:45 UTC

## Problem
A product can look excellent in a demonstration and still leave a first-time owner stranded at home. Ron proposes stores organized around things people want to do, with help close to the products.

## Decision
Proceed with the first two stores and the 25-store plan for this year. Steve wants a visitor to accomplish a task, not merely watch an employee operate a Mac. Phil will make the music station use the same library journey as the iTunes demo.

## Follow-up
