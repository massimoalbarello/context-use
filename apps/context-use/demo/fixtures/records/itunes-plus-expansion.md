# iTunes Plus expands and drops its price

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

Apple expands iTunes Plus to more than two million tracks and lowers all iTunes Plus songs to 99 cents. The DRM-free 256kbps AAC catalog includes independent labels alongside EMI.

Source: [iTunes Plus expands and drops its price](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2007/10/17iTunes-Plus-Now-Offers-Over-Two-Million-Tracks-at-Just-99-Cents/).
