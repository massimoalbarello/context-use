# iPhone specifications revised before sale

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

Apple raises advertised battery performance to up to eight hours of talk time and replaces the top plastic surface with optical-quality glass. US availability is June 29; battery claims depend on usage and network conditions.

Source: [iPhone specifications revised before sale](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2007/06/18iPhone-Delivers-Up-to-Eight-Hours-of-Talk-Time/).
