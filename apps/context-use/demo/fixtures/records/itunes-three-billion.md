# iTunes passes three billion song purchases

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

Apple reports more than three billion songs purchased and downloaded from iTunes. The store offers more than five million songs, 550 television shows and 500 movies, with integration across iPod and iPhone.

Source: [iTunes passes three billion song purchases](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2007/07/31iTunes-Store-Tops-Three-Billion-Songs/).
