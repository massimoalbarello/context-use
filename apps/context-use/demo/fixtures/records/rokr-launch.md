# Motorola ROKR launches with iTunes

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

The Motorola ROKR transfers up to 100 songs over USB from Mac or PC iTunes. Cingular offers the US phone at $249.99 with a two-year commitment; music pauses for calls.

Source: [Motorola ROKR launches with iTunes](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2005/09/07Apple-Motorola-Cingular-Launch-Worlds-First-Mobile-Phone-with-iTunes/).
