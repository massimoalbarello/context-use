# Shuffle review: remove the screen, move the burden carefully

Fictional demo · Synced from Granola · 2005-01-11

**Attendees:** Steve Jobs, Jony Ive, Tony Fadell, Phil Schiller\
**Time:** 09:00–09:45 UTC

## Decision
Ship the flash-based shuffle with simple playback controls and iTunes AutoFill. The announced options are 512MB at $99 and 1GB at $149.

## Tradeoff
Without a screen, a customer cannot browse the library on the device. AutoFill must make preparing music easy enough that the smaller, simpler player remains useful. Steve wants the demo to show that preparation instead of pretending the missing screen has no consequence.

## Actions
Tony owns testing AutoFill with an ordinary mixed library. Jony owns the physical controls; Phil explains who this player is for. Keep mini for customers who want to choose an album on the device. Simplicity is a choice about the whole workflow, not a count of removed parts.
