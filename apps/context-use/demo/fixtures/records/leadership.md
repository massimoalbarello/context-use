# Leadership handoff: make capacity and ownership explicit

Fictional demo · Synced from Granola · 2005-10-14

**Attendees:** Steve Jobs, Tim Cook, Jon Rubinstein, Tony Fadell\
**Time:** 09:00–09:45 UTC

## Changes
Tim becomes COO while continuing to lead the Macintosh Division. Jon plans to retire on March 31, 2006; Tony will take over iPod engineering and report to Steve.

## Risk discussed
The phone exploration, Intel transition and expanding iPod line all depend on experienced people. Steve does not want a role announcement to hide unresolved work or shared staffing assumptions.

## Handoff
Jon and Tony will walk the open engineering decisions and supplier dependencies together. Tim will keep allocation conflicts visible in operating reviews. Tony takes responsibility for the product family, not only the next launch. Bring disputed ownership to Steve before it becomes a missed date.
