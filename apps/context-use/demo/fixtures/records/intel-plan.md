# Mac transition: change the processor without losing the customer

Fictional demo · Synced from Granola · 2005-06-06

**Attendees:** Steve Jobs, Tim Cook, Phil Schiller, Scott Forstall\
**Time:** 09:00–09:45 UTC

## Commitment
Announce the first Intel Macs within a year and the complete Mac transition by the end of 2007. The Developer Transition Kit gives application teams a way to start preparing.

## Boundary
The customer buys a Mac to run applications and keep using their work. A faster processor is not a successful transition if the software they rely on stops being useful. Steve wants the developer story and the product schedule reviewed together.

## Owners
Tim owns the hardware transition sequence; Scott brings software readiness risks; Phil explains what customers can expect during the overlap. Keep this work visible beside iPod and the phone exploration so the same people are not implicitly promised to every launch.
