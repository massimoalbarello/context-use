# Mac Intel transition announced

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

At WWDC, Jobs announces the first Intel Macs within a year and a full Mac transition by the end of 2007. Developer Transition Kits help prepare applications for both processor families.

Source: [Mac Intel transition announced](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2005/06/06Apple-to-Use-Intel-Microprocessors-Beginning-in-2006/).
