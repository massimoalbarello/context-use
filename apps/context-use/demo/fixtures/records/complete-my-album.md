# iTunes introduces Complete My Album

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

Complete My Album gives customers a 99-cent credit for each previously purchased track toward a qualifying album. The initial offer allows completion within 180 days of the first individual song purchase.

Source: [iTunes introduces Complete My Album](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2007/03/29iTunes-Introduces-Complete-My-Album/).
