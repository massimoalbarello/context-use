# Making our store part of the music experience

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) have added [EMI's DRM-free option](context-use://page/opening-emi-purchases-beyond-drm) to a store that began with [individual songs](context-use://page/selling-songs-one-at-a-time), crossed into [Windows](context-use://page/bringing-our-own-itunes-to-windows) and grew into [television](context-use://page/working-with-bob-to-put-television-in-itunes). Each step answers a different obstacle to acquiring and using something people want.

[Complete My Album](context-use://page/completing-the-album) makes an earlier purchase count. The [Plus launch thread](context-use://record/itunes-plus) now raises a related question: does offering two versions at different prices make the next purchase harder to understand? [Ron](context-use://entity/ron-johnson) is collecting questions; [Phil](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) owns the explanation.

I want the next review to start with that evidence. A rights agreement is progress, but only the customer's task tells us whether we made things simpler.
