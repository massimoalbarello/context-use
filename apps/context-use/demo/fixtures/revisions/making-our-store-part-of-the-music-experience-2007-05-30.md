# Making our store part of the music experience

Our store has grown from [Mac song purchases](context-use://page/selling-songs-one-at-a-time) through [Windows](context-use://page/bringing-our-own-itunes-to-windows) into [television and shorts](context-use://page/working-with-bob-to-put-television-in-itunes). [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) am now adding [EMI’s DRM-free option](context-use://page/opening-emi-purchases-beyond-drm). Each step changes a different boundary: who can buy, what they can buy and where purchased files can play.
