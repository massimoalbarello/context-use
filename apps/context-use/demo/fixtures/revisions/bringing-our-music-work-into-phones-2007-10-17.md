# Bringing our music work into phones

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) can trace the iPhone work as a sequence of decisions rather than a launch date.

1. I accepted the [Motorola route](context-use://page/taking-itunes-onto-motorola-phones) to learn about music on phones. The [ROKR debrief](context-use://page/comparing-nano-and-rokr-with-tony) showed why improving one feature could not repair every seam.
2. I chose [fingers and software-defined controls](context-use://page/building-a-phone-around-our-fingers), then used the [Macworld rehearsal](context-use://page/walking-the-iphone-demonstration-with-scott) to prove transitions among music, calling and browsing.
3. The [January promise](context-use://page/introducing-the-phone-we-built) led through [the naming agreement](context-use://page/keeping-the-iphone-name) to [June readiness decisions](context-use://page/improving-iphone-before-customers-get-it) and [Ron's support handoff](context-use://page/preparing-the-first-customer-hour-with-ron). The [launch debrief](context-use://record/iphone-first-weekend) shows why the first customer hour needed ownership beyond the device.
4. The [holiday price cut](context-use://page/lowering-the-price-of-our-iphone) broadened access and created a trust problem. [The credit decision](context-use://page/responding-to-our-early-iphone-customers) responds to that evidence. [A million sales](context-use://page/passing-our-first-million-iphone-sales) closes a target, not the trust obligation.
5. The [web-only launch choice](context-use://page/asking-developers-to-build-for-the-web) met concrete limits in the [July feedback](context-use://record/developer-feedback). I have now [promised a native SDK for February](context-use://page/committing-to-native-applications-for-iphone), also for [iPod touch](context-use://page/bringing-the-phones-interface-back-to-ipod).

My current responsibility is to keep [Scott's](context-use://entity/scott-forstall) SDK scope, [Ron's](context-use://entity/ron-johnson) customer follow-through and [Phil's](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) public wording aligned. The original question remains useful: who can resolve the problem when the customer's task crosses a seam?
