# Taking our music experience beyond the Mac

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) can now follow the music journey through several decisions, each with a consequence. The [Musicmatch bridge](context-use://page/opening-ipod-to-windows-customers) expanded reach; [our own Windows software](context-use://page/bringing-our-own-itunes-to-windows) closed the library gap. [Mini](context-use://page/making-ipod-smaller-with-mini) made carrying easier, while its [revised date](context-use://page/revising-our-international-mini-schedule) exposed the cost of a weak supply promise.

[Shuffle](context-use://page/choosing-a-simpler-player-with-shuffle) moves selection into AutoFill; [nano](context-use://page/putting-a-display-into-the-smaller-nano) keeps album choice on a very small device. The [ROKR comparison](context-use://page/comparing-nano-and-rokr-with-tony) is now changing my phone direction. [Video iPod](context-use://page/giving-our-ipod-a-video-screen) and the [Disney agreement](context-use://page/working-with-bob-to-put-television-in-itunes) extend the journey to an episode.

The [current launch thread](context-use://record/video-ipod) asks [Tony](context-use://entity/tony-fadell) to prove purchase through playback. My constraint has stayed consistent since the [first rehearsal](context-use://record/demo-handoff): review the sequence the person performs, not only the parts we ship.
