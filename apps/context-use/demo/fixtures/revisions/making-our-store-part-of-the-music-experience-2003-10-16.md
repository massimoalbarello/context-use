# Making our store part of the music experience

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) have extended [the store we opened on Mac](context-use://page/selling-songs-one-at-a-time) to [our own Windows application](context-use://page/bringing-our-own-itunes-to-windows). Customers on either platform can move from buying a song into their library and onto an iPod. The [player’s earlier Windows support](context-use://page/opening-ipod-to-windows-customers) explains why this is a software-and-service milestone.
