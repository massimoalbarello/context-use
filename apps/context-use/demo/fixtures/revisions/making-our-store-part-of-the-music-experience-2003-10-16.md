The earlier [Musicmatch decision](context-use://page/opening-ipod-to-windows-customers) gave us reach before we owned that path. [Tony](context-use://entity/tony-fadell) now checks existing collections and [Ron](context-use://entity/ron-johnson) helps with the transition.
