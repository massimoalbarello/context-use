Historical asset credits — Steve Jobs, iPod to iPhone
Bundled images are public visual references; no authorship by Steve is implied.
Later portraits and iPhone/iPod touch reference images identify entities; they do not add later events to the 2001–2007 narrative.
Entity images are framed for circular avatars. Cropping, rotation, resizing and padding are identified below; image subjects have not been redrawn.
