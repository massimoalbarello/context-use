[Shuffle](context-use://page/choosing-a-simpler-player-with-shuffle) and [nano](context-use://page/putting-a-display-into-the-smaller-nano) make different control and storage tradeoffs. [Video playback](context-use://page/giving-our-ipod-a-video-screen) broadens what people can carry.

The [team that began this work](context-use://page/building-my-first-ipod-team) and the [first demo handoff](context-use://page/walking-the-first-ipod-demo-with-tony) remain my starting point.
