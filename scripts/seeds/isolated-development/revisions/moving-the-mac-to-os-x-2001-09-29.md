# Moving the Mac to OS X

By September 29, 2001, [my](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) [Mac OS X](context-use://entity/mac-os-x) transition has reached [10.1](context-use://page/making-os-x-more-useful-with-10-1). We have moved through the [preview](context-use://page/showing-the-next-mac-through-aqua), [beta](context-use://page/asking-customers-to-try-our-public-beta) and [March release](context-use://page/shipping-mac-os-x).

Our [May preinstallation step](context-use://page/putting-os-x-on-every-new-mac) put OS X beside OS 9.1 while leaving the older system as the default. [Avie Tevanian](context-use://entity/avie-tevanian) leads the software engineering behind this work, documented in Apple’s [later account](context-use://record/tevanian-history). With 10.1, we are improving performance and everyday usefulness as customers move across.
