# Moving the Mac to OS X

By September 29, 2001, [Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs)’s [Mac OS X](context-use://entity/mac-os-x) transition has reached [10.1](context-use://page/os-x-10-1-broadens-everyday-usefulness). The [preview](context-use://page/aqua-makes-the-next-mac-visible), [beta](context-use://page/listening-through-a-public-beta) and [March shipping](context-use://page/mac-os-x-reaches-customers) remain separate milestones.

The [May dual-boot decision](context-use://page/os-x-is-installed-but-os-9-still-boots) shows compatibility being managed in stages. [Avie Tevanian](context-use://entity/avie-tevanian)’s software leadership is established by a [later Apple retrospective](context-use://record/tevanian-history), not an invented contemporaneous memo. The release’s improved performance does not erase the earlier transition costs.
