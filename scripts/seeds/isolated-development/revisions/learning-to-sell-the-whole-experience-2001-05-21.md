# Learning to sell the whole experience

As of the May 21 report, [Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs)’s first two [Apple Retail Stores](context-use://entity/apple-retail-stores) have completed their [opening weekend](context-use://page/the-first-retail-weekend).

The [plan](context-use://page/the-first-stores-are-announced) now has a first observable result: visitors and merchandise sales across both locations. The result is encouraging, but opening-weekend interest cannot settle the economics of a national rollout.
