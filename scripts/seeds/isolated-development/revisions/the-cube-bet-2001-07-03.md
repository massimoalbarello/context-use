# The Cube bet

As of July 3, 2001, [Power Mac G4 Cube](context-use://entity/power-mac-g4-cube) production is [suspended indefinitely](context-use://page/the-cube-is-put-on-ice). The sequence is a [July launch](context-use://page/the-cube-joins-the-desktop-family), a [February music configuration](context-use://page/the-cube-turns-toward-cd-burning), then a halt.

This is a counterweight to [Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs)’s successful product narrative: a compelling physical idea still had to earn demand. [Schiller’s account](context-use://record/cube-ice) points to customers choosing towers; it does not establish one universal reason for every buyer’s choice.
