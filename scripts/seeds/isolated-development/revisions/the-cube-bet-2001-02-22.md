# The Cube bet

As of February 22, 2001, the [Power Mac G4 Cube](context-use://entity/power-mac-g4-cube) is still being developed. Its [CD-RW and iTunes update](context-use://page/the-cube-turns-toward-cd-burning) gives it a clearer role in music creation.

[Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs)’s [original compact-desktop bet](context-use://page/the-cube-joins-the-desktop-family) now has another configuration. That is evidence of an updated offer, not evidence that demand has recovered.
