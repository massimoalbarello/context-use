# The Mac as a digital hub

By October 23, [Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs)’s digital-media proposition has extended from [iTunes](context-use://page/music-becomes-a-mac-workflow) and [DVD creation](context-use://page/from-a-home-movie-to-a-dvd) to the [iPod announcement](context-use://page/a-music-library-in-the-hand).

The Mac organizes media; a connected device carries the result elsewhere. The [retail stores](context-use://page/learning-to-sell-the-whole-experience) provide a place to demonstrate those tasks. This synthesis describes the relationship among delivered products and announced plans, without claiming Steve had already foreseen their eventual scale.
