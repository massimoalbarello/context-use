# Two CEO roles

[Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) made his [Apple](context-use://entity/apple) CEO role permanent on January 5, 2000 while remaining chairman and CEO of [Pixar](context-use://entity/pixar), as the [contemporary report](context-use://record/ceo) records.

The period brings the [OS X transition](context-use://page/moving-the-mac-to-os-x) and [digital-hub products](context-use://page/the-mac-as-a-digital-hub) at Apple, and a [new Pixar home](context-use://page/a-new-home-for-pixar) and [Monsters, Inc. release](context-use://page/monsters-inc-reaches-an-audience) at Pixar. This is a picture of parallel responsibilities, not a claim to know his calendar or the division of his private attention.
