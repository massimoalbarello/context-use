# My two CEO roles

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) made my [Apple](context-use://entity/apple) CEO role permanent on January 5, 2000 while remaining chairman and CEO of [Pixar](context-use://entity/pixar). The [contemporary announcement](context-use://record/ceo) records that commitment.

At Apple, we have advanced the [OS X transition](context-use://page/moving-the-mac-to-os-x) and our [digital-hub products](context-use://page/the-mac-as-our-digital-hub). At Pixar, we have a [new home](context-use://page/our-new-home-in-emeryville) and have brought [Monsters, Inc. to audiences](context-use://page/bringing-monsters-inc-to-audiences). These are the two sets of responsibilities running through my work.
