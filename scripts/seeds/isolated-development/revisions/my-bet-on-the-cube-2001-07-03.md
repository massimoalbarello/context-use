# My bet on the Cube

As of July 3, 2001, we have [suspended production](context-use://page/putting-the-cube-on-ice) of the [Power Mac G4 Cube](context-use://entity/power-mac-g4-cube) indefinitely. [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) introduced it in [July](context-use://page/introducing-the-cube); we added a [music configuration](context-use://page/giving-the-cube-a-music-workflow) in February. We are now putting production on hold.

[Phil’s explanation](context-use://record/cube-ice) is that most customers chose the G4 minitower. Our compact design did not earn enough demand to sustain production. We have no announced plan for a new model.
