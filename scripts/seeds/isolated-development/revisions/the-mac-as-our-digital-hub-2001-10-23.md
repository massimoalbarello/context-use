# The Mac as our digital hub

By October 23, 2001, [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) have extended our digital-media work from [iTunes](context-use://page/bringing-music-into-the-mac) and [DVD creation](context-use://page/taking-a-home-movie-all-the-way-to-dvd) to the [iPod announcement](context-use://page/putting-a-music-library-in-your-hand).

The Mac organizes the media; a connected device carries the result elsewhere. Our [retail stores](context-use://page/showing-the-whole-experience-in-our-stores) give us a place to demonstrate those activities. We have shipped the software and announced the player; iPod’s planned availability is still ahead.
