# iPhone retail launch arrangements

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

Apple schedules US retail sales for June 29 at 6 p.m. local time. Ron Johnson describes hands-on demonstrations and support; the release lists 164 US Apple stores, free workshops and Genius Bar help.

Source: [iPhone retail launch arrangements](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2007/06/28iPhone-Premieres-This-Friday-Night-at-Apple-Retail-Stores/).
