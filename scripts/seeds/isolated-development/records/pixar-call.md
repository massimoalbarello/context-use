# Jobs and Iger discuss Pixar’s alternatives

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

In the acquisition conference call, Jobs describes a choice between pursuing a distribution arrangement and joining Disney. He emphasizes unifying the companies’ interests around films, stories and characters; Iger describes renewed conversations with Jobs.

Source: [Jobs and Iger discuss Pixar’s alternatives](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/app/uploads/ir/2006/events/pixar-2006-0124-transcript.pdf).
