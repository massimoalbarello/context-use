# Mac OS X 10.1 availability and improvements

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

Apple announced September 29 availability, improved performance and broader digital-media/device support. A limited free retail upgrade kit differed from the $19.95 shipping-and-handling Up-to-Date program. Full retail price remained $129.

Source: [Mac OS X 10.1 availability and improvements](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2001/09/25First-Major-Upgrade-to-Mac-OS-X-Hits-Stores-This-Weekend/).
