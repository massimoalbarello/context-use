# Fiscal Q1 2001 loss and inventory reset

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

For the quarter ended December 30, 2000, Apple reported a $195 million net loss and $1 billion revenue. Jobs said channel inventory was about five and a half weeks. CFO Fred Anderson cited over $4 billion cash and a planned return to profitability.

Source: [Fiscal Q1 2001 loss and inventory reset](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2001/01/17Apple-Reports-First-Quarter-Results/).
