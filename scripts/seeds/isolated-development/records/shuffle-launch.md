# Flash storage and AutoFill in iPod shuffle

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

The USB flash player launches in 512MB/$99 and 1GB/$149 versions. Users select ordered or shuffled playback; iTunes AutoFill chooses music for the device.

Source: [Flash storage and AutoFill in iPod shuffle](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2005/01/11Apple-Introduces-iPod-shuffle/).
