# Video comes to iPod hardware

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

The new 30GB/$299 and 60GB/$399 iPods add video playback on a 2.5-inch color screen. Apple plans shipping for the following week, alongside iTunes 6.

Source: [Video comes to iPod hardware](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2005/10/12Apple-Unveils-the-New-iPod/).
