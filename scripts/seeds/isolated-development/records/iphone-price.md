# iPhone price reduction

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

The 8GB iPhone drops from $599 to $399 immediately in the US. The 4GB version remains only while supplies last. The release forecasts a million sales by month-end, rather than claiming they have already occurred.
