# First physical stores and 25-store plan

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

Apple announced May 19 openings at Tysons Corner in McLean and Glendale Galleria. Its 2001 target was 25 stores. Demonstrations, digital-lifestyle solutions and a Genius Bar were central to the format; these were plans before opening day.

Source: [First physical stores and 25-store plan](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2001/05/15Apple-to-Open-25-Retail-Stores-in-2001/).
