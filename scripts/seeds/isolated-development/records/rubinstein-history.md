# Rubinstein recalls the first iPod team

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

In his later CHM oral history (especially printed pages 87–91), Rubinstein recalls assembling a cross-functional team, with Tony Fadell leading it, to ship iPod in under a year. He credits Ive’s team with industrial design and Schiller with the scroll-wheel idea (printed page 89). This is retrospective testimony; recollections about timing are not contemporaneous meeting minutes.

Source: [Rubinstein recalls the first iPod team](https://archive.computerhistory.org/resources/access/text/2020/02/102717908-05-01-acc.pdf).
