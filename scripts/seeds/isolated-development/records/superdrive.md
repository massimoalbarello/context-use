# SuperDrive systems begin shipping

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

Apple began shipping the 733 MHz Power Mac G4 with a drive that read and wrote CDs and DVDs. The bundle included iTunes, iMovie 2 and iDVD, joining import, editing and disc creation in one desktop workflow.

Source: [SuperDrive systems begin shipping](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2001/02/19Apple-Ships-Industrys-First-SuperDrive/).
