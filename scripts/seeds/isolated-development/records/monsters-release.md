# Monsters Inc US release and creative credits

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

The film’s US release was November 2, 2001. Pete Docter directed, with David Silverman and Lee Unkrich credited in directing roles; Andrew Stanton and Dan Gerson wrote the screenplay. Steve Jobs was a studio executive, not the film’s director.

Source: [Monsters Inc US release and creative credits](https://movies.disney.com/monsters-inc).
