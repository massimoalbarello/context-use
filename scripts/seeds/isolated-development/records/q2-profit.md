# Fiscal Q2 2001 return to profit

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

For the quarter ended March 31, Apple reported $43 million net profit and $1.43 billion revenue. Revenue remained 26 percent below the year-earlier quarter. Excluding specified non-recurring items, profit was $40 million.

Source: [Fiscal Q2 2001 return to profit](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2001/04/18Apple-Reports-Second-Quarter-Profit-of-43-Million/).
