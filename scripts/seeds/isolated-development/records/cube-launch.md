# Cube and iMovie 2 desktop announcement

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

Apple presented the eight-inch G4 Cube in a clear enclosure alongside new iMacs and dual-processor Power Macs. The Cube had a 450 MHz G4 and a top-loading DVD drive. iMovie 2 accompanied the new desktop lineup.

Source: [Cube and iMovie 2 desktop announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2000/07/19Apple-Unveils-Entirely-New-Desktop-Line-Including-the-Revolutionary-Power-Mac-G4-Cube/).
