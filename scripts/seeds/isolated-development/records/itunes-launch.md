# iTunes turns the Mac into a music library

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

The free Mac application imported CDs, encoded MP3s, organized playlists and burned audio CDs. It required Mac OS 9.0.4 or 9.1. This was local music-library software, not an online music store.

Source: [iTunes turns the Mac into a music library](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2001/01/09Apple-Introduces-iTunes-Worlds-Best-and-Easiest-To-Use-Jukebox-Software/).
