# Music Store opens on the Mac

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

The US store launches inside iTunes 4 for Mac with over 200,000 songs from five major labels. Individual tracks cost 99 cents, without a subscription; purchased AAC files carry usage restrictions.

Source: [Music Store opens on the Mac](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2003/04/28Apple-Launches-the-iTunes-Music-Store/).
