# Fiscal Q4 closes before iPod announcement

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

Apple reported $66 million net profit and $1.45 billion revenue for the quarter ended September 29. Jobs cited OS X, education iBook sales and retail openings. The period ended before iPod was announced, so these results cannot measure its sales.

Source: [Fiscal Q4 closes before iPod announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2001/10/17Apple-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-Profit-of-66-Million/).
