# Cube production suspended indefinitely

Research summary, paraphrased for this seed; not the original document.

Apple suspended Cube production indefinitely with no planned replacement. Phil Schiller said most customers chose Power Mac G4 minitowers. Apple left a small possibility of an upgraded Cube; suspension was not a promised relaunch.

Source: [Cube production suspended indefinitely](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2001/07/03Apple-Puts-Power-Mac-G4-Cube-on-Ice/).
