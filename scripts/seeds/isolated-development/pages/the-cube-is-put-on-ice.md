# The Cube is put on ice

On July 3, [Apple](context-use://entity/apple) suspended [Power Mac G4 Cube](context-use://entity/power-mac-g4-cube) production indefinitely. [Phil Schiller](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) said most customers chose the G4 minitower instead.

The [announcement](seed-record:cube-ice) leaves a small possibility of a future model but no plan. For [Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs), the [Cube bet](context-use://page/the-cube-bet) reaches a commercial limit even after the [music update](context-use://page/the-cube-turns-toward-cd-burning). The source does not say it was recalled.
