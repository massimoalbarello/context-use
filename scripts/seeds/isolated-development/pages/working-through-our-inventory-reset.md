# Working through our inventory reset

For the quarter ending December 30, 2000, we reported a $195 million net loss on $1 billion in revenue. [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) reported about five and a half weeks of channel inventory. [Fred Anderson](context-use://entity/fred-anderson) emphasized our cash position and the intention to return to profitability.

Our [January 17 results](context-use://record/q1-loss) describe the first fiscal quarter of 2001, although it ended in calendar 2000. We have reduced inventory, but this quarter’s financial result is a substantial setback.
