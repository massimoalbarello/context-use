# The Cube bet

As of July 19, 2000, the [Cube unveiling](context-use://page/the-cube-joins-the-desktop-family) makes [Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs)’s desktop lineup more varied. The [Power Mac G4 Cube](context-use://entity/power-mac-g4-cube) offers compactness and a distinctive enclosure beside conventional towers.

The open commercial question is whether that distinction gives customers enough reason to choose it. The launch announcement supplies specifications, not evidence of sustained demand.
