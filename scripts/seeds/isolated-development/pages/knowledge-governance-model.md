# Knowledge governance model

[Alex Morgan](context-use://entity/alex-morgan), [Jun Park](context-use://entity/jun-park), and [Priya Shah](context-use://entity/priya-shah) defined ownership for knowledge connected through [Compass](context-use://entity/compass).

## Responsibilities

Authors maintain current pages, reviewers preserve evidence, and workspace owners archive obsolete resources without breaking history. Operational details live in the [content lifecycle policy](context-use://page/content-lifecycle-policy).
