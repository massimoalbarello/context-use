# The first retail weekend

The [Tysons Corner](context-use://entity/tysons-corner) and [Glendale Galleria](context-use://entity/glendale-galleria) stores opened May 19. Across May 19–20, they welcomed over 7,700 visitors and sold $599,000 combined.

[Apple’s May 21 report](seed-record:stores-weekend) gives [Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) an early result for the [retail plan](context-use://page/the-first-stores-are-announced). Neither figure belongs to one store alone, and neither establishes long-term store profitability.
