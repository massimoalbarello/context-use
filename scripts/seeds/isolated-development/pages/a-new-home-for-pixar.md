# A new home for Pixar

[Pixar](context-use://entity/pixar)’s move to the [Emeryville Campus](context-use://entity/emeryville-campus) belongs to the same period in which [Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) is taking permanent responsibility at Apple. [Ed Catmull](context-use://entity/ed-catmull) represents the studio’s continuing technical and creative leadership.

Pixar’s [retrospective timeline](context-use://record/pixar-campus) places the move in its 2000–2001 section, without an exact day. This page keeps that broad interval rather than assigning a fictional moving date. The new building is a setting for the studio’s work, not proof that architecture caused a film’s success.
