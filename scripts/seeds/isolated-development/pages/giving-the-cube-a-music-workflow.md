# Giving the Cube a music workflow

At Macworld Tokyo on February 22, 2001, we introduced a [Power Mac G4 Cube](context-use://entity/power-mac-g4-cube) with CD-RW and [iTunes](context-use://entity/itunes). Our [Apple](context-use://entity/apple) [configuration announcement](context-use://record/cube-cdrw) updates the optical drive from the [original DVD model](context-use://page/introducing-the-cube).

The machine can now support the CD-import, playlist and CD-burning workflow [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) presented in January. We have changed the offer; this announcement does not yet tell us how customers will respond.
