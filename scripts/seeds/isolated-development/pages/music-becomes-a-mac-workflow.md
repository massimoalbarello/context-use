# Music becomes a Mac workflow

On January 9, [Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) introduced [iTunes](context-use://entity/itunes): import CDs, organize songs and playlists, then burn a personal audio CD. The [release record](context-use://record/itunes-launch) describes a free Mac OS 9 application.

This makes music a complete task on the Mac, complementing the video work already served by [iMovie](context-use://entity/imovie). There is no online music shop in this announcement.
