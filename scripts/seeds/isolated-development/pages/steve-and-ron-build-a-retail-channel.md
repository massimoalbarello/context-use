# Steve and Ron build a retail channel

[Ron Johnson](context-use://entity/ron-johnson) joined [Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) in creating [Apple Retail Stores](context-use://entity/apple-retail-stores). The [later memoir description](context-use://record/johnson-history) dates his hiring to 2000.

Their work becomes publicly observable in the [May store announcement](context-use://page/the-first-stores-are-announced) and [opening-weekend result](context-use://page/the-first-retail-weekend). The [rehearsal page](context-use://page/rehearsing-one-complete-customer-task) is an explicitly fictional illustration of the format, not an additional source for their relationship.
