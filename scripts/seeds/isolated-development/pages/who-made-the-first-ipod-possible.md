# Who made the first iPod possible

[Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) presented [iPod](context-use://entity/ipod), but development was a team effort. [Jon Rubinstein](context-use://entity/jon-rubinstein)’s [later oral history](seed-record:rubinstein-history) describes assembling the effort and [Tony Fadell](context-use://entity/tony-fadell) leading the team. CHM’s [Fadell interview account](seed-record:fadell-history) also places his entry at Apple in 2001.

Rubinstein credits [Jonathan Ive](context-use://entity/jonathan-ive)’s team with the industrial design and [Phil Schiller](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) with proposing a scroll wheel after seeing one on a Bang & Olufsen phone. These details locate contributions beyond the person presenting on stage. These are retrospective attributions with uncertain fine-grained timing; the [October announcement](context-use://page/a-music-library-in-the-hand) is the contemporary product anchor.
