# My two CEO roles

On January 5, 2000, [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) made my [Apple](context-use://entity/apple) CEO role permanent. I am also chairman and CEO of [Pixar](context-use://entity/pixar). Dropping “interim” makes my commitment to Apple explicit while I continue leading the studio.

The [announcement report](context-use://record/ceo) records both responsibilities. My work now spans the Mac business and an animation studio, each with its own team and projects.
