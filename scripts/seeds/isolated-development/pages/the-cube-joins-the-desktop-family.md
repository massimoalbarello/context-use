# The Cube joins the desktop family

On July 19, [Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) introduced the [Power Mac G4 Cube](context-use://entity/power-mac-g4-cube) alongside new iMacs and dual-processor Power Macs. Its small clear enclosure offered another expression of the Mac, rather than replacing every desktop.

The [launch record](seed-record:cube-launch) identifies a 450 MHz G4 and a DVD drive. [iMovie](context-use://entity/imovie) 2 connected the new hardware to making home movies. The appeal of an object and demand for that object will need to be judged separately.
