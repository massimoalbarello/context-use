# Making OS X more useful with 10.1

On September 25, 2001, we announced [Mac OS X](context-use://entity/mac-os-x) 10.1 for September 29 availability. [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) am bringing customers a release with better performance and broader digital-media support. Our [upgrade announcement](context-use://record/osx-101) distinguishes the limited free retail kit from the mailed upgrade with a handling charge.

We also reported [more than 1,400 third-party applications](context-use://record/osx-apps). Applications and device support matter alongside the operating system: customers need to be able to do their everyday work on the new platform.
