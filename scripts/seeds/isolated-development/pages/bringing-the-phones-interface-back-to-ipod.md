# Bringing the phone’s interface back to iPod

With [iPod touch](context-use://entity/ipod-touch), [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) am bringing our phone’s touch interface and Wi-Fi into the player family. Our [announcement](context-use://record/touch-launch) includes Safari and wireless store purchases; 8GB/$299 and 16GB/$399 models are planned for later in September.

The relationship now runs in both directions: [iPod led into iPhone](context-use://page/bringing-our-music-work-into-phones), and iPhone’s interface is changing iPod.
