# Our first retail weekend

Our [Tysons Corner](context-use://entity/tysons-corner) and [Glendale Galleria](context-use://entity/glendale-galleria) stores opened on May 19, 2001. Over May 19–20, the two locations welcomed more than 7,700 visitors and sold $599,000 combined.

The [May 21 results](context-use://record/stores-weekend) give [me](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) an early measure of our [retail plan](context-use://page/announcing-our-first-stores). We have opening-weekend traffic and sales; we still need evidence about the economics of a wider rollout.
