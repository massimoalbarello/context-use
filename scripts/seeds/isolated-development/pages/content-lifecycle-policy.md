# Content lifecycle policy

[Alex Morgan](context-use://entity/alex-morgan), [Maya Chen](context-use://entity/maya-chen), and [Theo Brooks](context-use://entity/theo-brooks) maintain resources connected through [Compass](context-use://entity/compass).

## Lifecycle

Create knowledge for a concrete decision, revise it as evidence changes, and archive it when it no longer informs current work. Follow the [knowledge governance model](context-use://page/knowledge-governance-model) and preserve review history in the [decision log](context-use://page/decision-log).
