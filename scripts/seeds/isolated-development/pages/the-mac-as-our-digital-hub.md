# The Mac as our digital hub

As of January 9, 2001, [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) am expanding the Mac’s role in everyday media. Our new [music application](context-use://page/bringing-music-into-the-mac) joins [iMovie](context-use://entity/imovie)’s home-video editing.

The digital-hub proposition puts the Mac at the center of organizing and creating with connected devices. So far, the concrete work here is in music and video software; we have not announced a portable Apple music player.
