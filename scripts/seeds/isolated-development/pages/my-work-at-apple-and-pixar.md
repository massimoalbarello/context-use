# My work at Apple and Pixar

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) lead [Apple](context-use://entity/apple) and [Pixar](context-use://entity/pixar), with [two CEO responsibilities](context-use://page/my-two-ceo-roles) and separate teams. At Apple, I work with [our product and retail leaders](context-use://page/my-collaborators-at-apple) on the Mac, software, stores and iPod.

At Pixar, I am chairman and CEO. [Pete Docter](context-use://entity/pete-docter) directs [Monsters, Inc.](context-use://entity/monsters-inc), and [Disney](context-use://entity/disney) is our distribution partner. The [film credits](context-use://record/monsters-release) and [production account](context-use://record/monsters-craft) identify the people doing the creative work.

Our [Emeryville campus](context-use://page/our-new-home-in-emeryville) and [film release](context-use://page/bringing-monsters-inc-to-audiences) sit alongside my Apple projects. My studio leadership and Pete’s film direction are different responsibilities.
