# Reaching one hundred million iPods

Our [sales announcement](context-use://record/ipod-100m) reports 100 million [iPods](context-use://entity/ipod) sold across the family. [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) can compare that scale with [the first player we introduced](context-use://page/putting-the-first-ipod-in-my-pocket), without attributing the entire total to that original model.

This is cumulative iPod volume as we prepare [iPhone](context-use://page/introducing-the-phone-we-built), not a phone-sales result.
