# Accessibility audit

[Maya Chen](context-use://entity/maya-chen), [Priya Shah](context-use://entity/priya-shah), and [Theo Brooks](context-use://entity/theo-brooks) audited the [Northstar](context-use://entity/northstar) map with keyboard and screen-reader workflows.

## Findings

Resource type cannot rely on shape or color alone. Every interactive region needs a stable accessible name, and previews must remain reachable without hover. Track fixes in the [UI review checklist](context-use://page/ui-review-checklist).
