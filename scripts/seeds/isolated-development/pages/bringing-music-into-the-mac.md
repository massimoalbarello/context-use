# Bringing music into the Mac

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) introduced [iTunes](context-use://entity/itunes) as a free way to import CDs, organize a music library and burn personal audio CDs on the [Mac](context-use://entity/mac). This gives our customers a complete local music workflow.

Our [launch announcement](context-use://record/itunes-launch) describes the application. We are introducing music-library software here; the download store comes later in this story.
