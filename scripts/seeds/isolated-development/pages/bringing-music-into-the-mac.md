# Bringing music into the Mac

On January 9, 2001, [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) introduced [iTunes](context-use://entity/itunes). People can import CDs, organize songs and playlists, and burn a personal audio CD with one free Mac OS 9 application. Our [release announcement](context-use://record/itunes-launch) describes that workflow.

We already have [iMovie](context-use://entity/imovie) for home video. iTunes gives the Mac a corresponding role in music. This release manages a local music library; we have not announced an online music shop.
