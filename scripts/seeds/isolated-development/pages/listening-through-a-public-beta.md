# Listening through a public beta

At Apple Expo Paris on September 13, [Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) invited customers to try [Mac OS X](context-use://entity/mac-os-x). The [public beta](seed-record:osx-beta) cost $29.95 and supported English, French and German.

The earlier [Aqua preview](context-use://page/aqua-makes-the-next-mac-visible) had targeted a summer commercial release. September brought a preview for feedback. Keeping both moments makes the schedule change visible without inventing its internal cause.
