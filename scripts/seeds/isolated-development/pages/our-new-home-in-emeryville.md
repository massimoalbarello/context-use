# Our new home in Emeryville

At [Pixar](context-use://entity/pixar), we are moving into our [Emeryville Campus](context-use://entity/emeryville-campus). [Ed Catmull](context-use://entity/ed-catmull) is my colleague in the studio’s technical and creative leadership. This is happening alongside [my](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) continuing work at Apple.

The campus brings a new physical setting to our film work. Pixar’s [retrospective timeline](context-use://record/pixar-campus) places the move within 2000–2001; the available account does not establish an exact moving day.
