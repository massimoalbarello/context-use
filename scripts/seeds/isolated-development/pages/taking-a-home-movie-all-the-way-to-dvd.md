# Taking a home movie all the way to DVD

On February 19, 2001, we began shipping SuperDrive-equipped Power Mac G4 systems at [Apple](context-use://entity/apple), with [iMovie](context-use://entity/imovie), [iDVD](context-use://entity/idvd) and [iTunes](context-use://entity/itunes). Our [shipping announcement](context-use://record/superdrive) connects the software to hardware that can write the finished disc.

[My](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) [digital-hub work](context-use://page/the-mac-as-our-digital-hub) now covers the complete movie task: bring video into the Mac, edit it and make a DVD to play away from the computer.
