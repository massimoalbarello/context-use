# Improving iPhone before customers get it

Before launch, we have [revised iPhone’s specifications](context-use://record/iphone-glass): a glass top surface replaces plastic, and advertised talk time is now up to eight hours. Actual battery results depend on conditions.

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) am updating what we can deliver after [the January unveiling](context-use://page/introducing-the-phone-we-built). We now name June 29 for the US sale; the announced 4GB and 8GB price points are unchanged at this checkpoint.
