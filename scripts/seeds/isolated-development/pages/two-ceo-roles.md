# Two CEO roles

On January 5, [Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) dropped “interim” from his [Apple](context-use://entity/apple) CEO title while continuing to lead [Pixar](context-use://entity/pixar). The commitment connects two demanding businesses; it does not establish how he divided each working day.

The [contemporary report](context-use://record/ceo) preserves the announcement. This account follows his public work rather than supplying an imagined private diary.
