# Steve’s circle of collaborators

[Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) is the connecting person in this workspace, but a product bearing [Apple](context-use://entity/apple)’s name is not evidence that he designed every part of it. This reference note distinguishes the people a reader will encounter; the linked project pages hold the chronology.

[Avie Tevanian](context-use://entity/avie-tevanian) connects Steve to the [Mac OS X transition](context-use://page/moving-the-mac-to-os-x). Apple’s [later account of his software leadership](context-use://record/tevanian-history) supports that responsibility. [Jon Rubinstein](context-use://entity/jon-rubinstein) and [Tony Fadell](context-use://entity/tony-fadell) connect him to [the first iPod team](context-use://page/who-made-the-first-ipod-possible). Rubinstein’s [oral history](context-use://record/rubinstein-history) also credits [Jonathan Ive](context-use://entity/jonathan-ive)’s design team and [Phil Schiller](context-use://entity/phil-schiller)’s scroll-wheel suggestion.

[Ron Johnson](context-use://entity/ron-johnson) is the collaborator to follow for [the retail channel](context-use://page/steve-and-ron-build-a-retail-channel). These are specific working relationships supported by the cited accounts, not an invented organization chart or a claim about Steve’s private friendships.
