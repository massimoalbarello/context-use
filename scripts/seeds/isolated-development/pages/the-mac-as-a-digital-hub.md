# The Mac as a digital hub

As of January 9, 2001, [Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs)’s Mac proposition is taking shape around everyday media: [music management](context-use://page/music-becomes-a-mac-workflow) joins [iMovie](context-use://entity/imovie)’s home-video editing.

“Digital hub” here describes the connected workflow: the Mac helps people organize and create with their devices. The concrete evidence is the software and what a user can do with it; no portable Apple music player has been announced in this chapter yet.
