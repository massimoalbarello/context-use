# The products we make and how people use them

[My](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) product work brings together the physical object and the task it enables. The [Power Mac G4 Cube](context-use://entity/power-mac-g4-cube) gives us a compact desktop; [iPod](context-use://entity/ipod) puts a music library in someone’s hand.

The [Cube image](context-use://asset/power-mac-g4-cube-photo) records its form. Our [Cube project notes](context-use://page/my-bet-on-the-cube) hold the separate commercial history: launch, a new music configuration and the production halt. The photograph alone does not tell us why customers chose other machines.

On iPod, [Jonathan Ive](context-use://entity/jonathan-ive)’s team shaped the industrial design, and [Phil Schiller](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) contributed the scroll-wheel suggestion, according to [Jon Rubinstein](context-use://entity/jon-rubinstein)’s [later account](context-use://record/rubinstein-history). The [player image](context-use://asset/original-ipod-photo) and our [music workflow](context-use://page/our-digital-hub-model) connect the object with what people do with it.
