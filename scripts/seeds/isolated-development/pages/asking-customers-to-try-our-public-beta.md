# Asking customers to try our public beta

At Apple Expo Paris on September 13, 2000, [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) invited customers to try [Mac OS X](context-use://entity/mac-os-x) and send feedback. Our [public beta](context-use://record/osx-beta) costs $29.95 and supports English, French and German.

At the [Aqua preview](context-use://page/showing-the-next-mac-through-aqua), we had targeted a summer commercial release. We are now offering a beta rather than the finished system. Feedback from people using their own applications is the next input to the transition.
