# A financial checkpoint before iPod

[Apple](context-use://entity/apple) reported $66 million quarterly net profit on October 17 for the period ended September 29. [Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) cited software, education and retail progress in the [results record](seed-record:q4-profit).

Alongside the [earlier return to profit](context-use://page/quarterly-profit-returns), the [selected results CSV](context-use://asset/apple-quarterly-results) makes recovery inspectable. Its period-end and report-date columns serve different purposes. This quarter ended before the iPod announcement and cannot be used as iPod sales evidence.
