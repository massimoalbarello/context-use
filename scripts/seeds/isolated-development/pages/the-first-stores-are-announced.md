# The first stores are announced

On May 15, [Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) announced the first [Apple Retail Stores](context-use://entity/apple-retail-stores), to open May 19 at [Tysons Corner](context-use://entity/tysons-corner) and [Glendale Galleria](context-use://entity/glendale-galleria).

The [announcement](seed-record:stores-plan) emphasizes demonstrations, help at the Genius Bar and digital-media tasks. For Steve, the store is a way to make the [Mac’s usefulness](context-use://page/the-mac-as-a-digital-hub) visible. Twenty-five stores is the stated year-end plan, not a count already open.
