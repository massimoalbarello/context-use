# Putting a music library in your hand

On October 23, 2001, [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) announced [iPod](context-use://entity/ipod), connecting a pocket player to our [iTunes music library](context-use://page/bringing-music-into-the-mac). Our [launch announcement](context-use://record/ipod-launch) specifies FireWire Auto-Sync, 5 GB of storage and a $399 US price.

We have announced November 10 availability. The scroll wheel lets someone navigate a large library with one hand, while synchronization connects the music on the player to its organization on the Mac.
