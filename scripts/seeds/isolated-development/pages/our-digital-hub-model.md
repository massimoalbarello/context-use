# Our digital-hub model

[My](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) digital-hub work puts the Mac at the center of creating, organizing and using personal media. The [project notes](context-use://page/the-mac-as-our-digital-hub) track how we are building out that role.

With [iTunes](context-use://entity/itunes), we give people a music library and playlists on the Mac. [iPod](context-use://entity/ipod) carries that music away from the desk. Our [iTunes announcement](context-use://record/itunes-launch) describes importing and burning CDs; our [iPod announcement](context-use://record/ipod-launch) adds FireWire transfer and synchronization. Together, the software and player support one activity across two devices.

We have a parallel path for movies: [iMovie](context-use://entity/imovie) for editing and [iDVD](context-use://entity/idvd) for [making the finished disc](context-use://page/taking-a-home-movie-all-the-way-to-dvd). [Mac OS X](context-use://entity/mac-os-x) is our operating-system foundation. The media applications are the tasks we build on the Mac, not substitutes for that foundation.
