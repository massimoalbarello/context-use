# Opening EMI purchases beyond DRM

We have launched iTunes Plus with [EMI](context-use://entity/emi): 256kbps AAC tracks without DRM at $1.29 each. Our [release](context-use://record/itunes-plus) also retains the existing 99-cent protected versions.

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) am changing part of [our store’s purchase model](context-use://page/making-our-store-part-of-the-music-experience), not claiming the whole catalog is now unrestricted. The distinction matters when people ask what can play on another device.
