# My collaborators at Apple

[My](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) work at [Apple](context-use://entity/apple) brings together software, hardware, industrial design, marketing and retail. Different colleagues carry different parts of that work.

[Avie Tevanian](context-use://entity/avie-tevanian) leads the software engineering behind our [Mac OS X transition](context-use://page/moving-the-mac-to-os-x), as Apple’s [later account of his leadership](context-use://record/tevanian-history) records. [Jon Rubinstein](context-use://entity/jon-rubinstein) and [Tony Fadell](context-use://entity/tony-fadell) are central to [our first iPod team](context-use://page/the-team-behind-our-first-ipod). Jon’s [oral history](context-use://record/rubinstein-history) credits [Jonathan Ive](context-use://entity/jonathan-ive)’s team with industrial design and [Phil Schiller](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) with the scroll-wheel suggestion.

[Ron Johnson](context-use://entity/ron-johnson) is my partner in [building the retail channel](context-use://page/building-our-retail-channel-with-ron). These relationships connect the products we make to how customers encounter and use them.
