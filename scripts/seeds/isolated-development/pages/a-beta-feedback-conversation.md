# A beta feedback conversation

**Synthetic scenario — the conversation, date and attendance are invented.**

The exercise makes the real [public-beta feedback request](context-use://page/listening-through-a-public-beta) tangible. It does not establish an actual meeting, a private instruction from Steve or a reason for the release schedule.
