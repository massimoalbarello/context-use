# Helping customers in our stores

[Ron Johnson](context-use://entity/ron-johnson) and [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) are bringing hardware, software and help together in [Apple Retail Stores](context-use://entity/apple-retail-stores). Customers can see a complete task demonstrated and get help with it.

Our [store-format announcement](context-use://record/stores-plan) makes demonstrations, digital-lifestyle solutions and the Genius Bar central to the experience. That gives our [digital-hub work](context-use://page/our-digital-hub-model) a physical place: people can see what the connected products let them do.

[Tysons Corner](context-use://entity/tysons-corner) and [Glendale Galleria](context-use://entity/glendale-galleria) are our first locations. The [retail project notes](context-use://page/showing-the-whole-experience-in-our-stores) track our opening plan and the first reported results.
