# OS X 10.1 broadens everyday usefulness

Apple announced [Mac OS X](context-use://entity/mac-os-x) 10.1 on September 25 for September 29 availability, emphasizing performance and digital-media support. The [upgrade record](seed-record:osx-101) distinguishes a limited free retail kit from a mailed upgrade with a handling charge.

The separate [application count](seed-record:osx-apps) reports more than 1,400 third-party titles. For [Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs), the platform’s usefulness depends on applications and devices as well as the operating system itself.
