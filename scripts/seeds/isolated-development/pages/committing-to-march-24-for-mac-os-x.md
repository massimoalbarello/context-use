# Committing to March 24 for Mac OS X

On January 9, 2001, [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) announced that [Apple](context-use://entity/apple) would make [Mac OS X](context-use://entity/mac-os-x) available on March 24 for $129. Our [announcement](context-use://record/osx-date) reports more than 100,000 beta copies and 75,000 feedback entries.

The [public beta](context-use://page/asking-customers-to-try-our-public-beta) has given us substantial customer input. We now have a specific delivery commitment; the participation figures alone do not settle every compatibility question.
