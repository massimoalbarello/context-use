# Putting the first iPod in my pocket

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) introduced our [iPod](context-use://entity/ipod): 5GB, a mechanical scroll wheel and a $399 US price. It connects to a [Mac](context-use://entity/mac) over FireWire and synchronizes with [iTunes](context-use://entity/itunes).

Our [announcement](context-use://record/ipod-launch) gives November 10 as planned availability. October 23 is the unveiling, not the first retail-sales day. This builds on [our music-library software](context-use://page/bringing-music-into-the-mac) and the work of [my iPod team](context-use://page/building-my-first-ipod-team).
