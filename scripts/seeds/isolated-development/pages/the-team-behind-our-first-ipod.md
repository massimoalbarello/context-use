# The team behind our first iPod

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) presented [iPod](context-use://entity/ipod), with [Jon Rubinstein](context-use://entity/jon-rubinstein) assembling the effort and [Tony Fadell](context-use://entity/tony-fadell) leading the development team. Jon’s [later oral history](context-use://record/rubinstein-history) describes that work, and the [Fadell interview account](context-use://record/fadell-history) places Tony’s arrival at Apple in 2001.

[Jonathan Ive](context-use://entity/jonathan-ive)’s team handled industrial design. Jon credits [Phil Schiller](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) with the scroll-wheel suggestion after seeing one on a Bang & Olufsen phone. These are the people behind the product I [announced in October](context-use://page/putting-a-music-library-in-your-hand); the detailed contribution accounts come from later recollections.
