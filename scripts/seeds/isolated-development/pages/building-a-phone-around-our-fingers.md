# Building a phone around our fingers

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) work with [Scott Forstall](context-use://entity/scott-forstall) on a phone whose interface changes with the task. The team pursues fingers and a large touch display instead of fixing the interface around a physical keyboard.

[Scott’s later interview and the engineers’ recollections](context-use://record/forstall-history) describe our software leadership and secret recruitment. They do not supply an exact date for the initial decision, so I do not turn it into a dated meeting. The [ROKR collaboration](context-use://page/shipping-itunes-on-the-rokr) is a separate strand of our phone story.
