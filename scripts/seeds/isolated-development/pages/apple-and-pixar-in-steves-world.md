# Apple and Pixar in Steve’s world

[Apple](context-use://entity/apple) and [Pixar](context-use://entity/pixar) belong in the same personal graph because of [Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs). They remain separate organizations with different products and creative responsibilities. The [two-CEO account](context-use://page/two-ceo-roles) dates his overlapping leadership; this note is a guide to the distinction, not a claim that he held both jobs indefinitely.

At Apple, follow Steve through [the people building the products](context-use://page/steves-circle-of-collaborators). At Pixar, distinguish studio leadership from making a particular film: [Pete Docter](context-use://entity/pete-docter) directed [Monsters, Inc.](context-use://entity/monsters-inc), and [Disney](context-use://entity/disney) connects to its release. The [film credits](context-use://record/monsters-release) and [production account](context-use://record/monsters-craft) support those separate roles.

[The Emeryville campus](context-use://page/a-new-home-for-pixar) gives the studio a physical setting. [The film’s release](context-use://page/monsters-inc-reaches-an-audience) is a dated event within that world. Neither makes the film an Apple project or Steve its director.
