# Announcing our first stores

On May 15, 2001, [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) announced our first [Apple Retail Stores](context-use://entity/apple-retail-stores). We plan to open at [Tysons Corner](context-use://entity/tysons-corner) and [Glendale Galleria](context-use://entity/glendale-galleria) on May 19.

Our [announcement](context-use://record/stores-plan) puts demonstrations, Genius Bar help and digital-media tasks at the center of the format. The stores give us a place to show [what people can do with a Mac](context-use://page/the-mac-as-our-digital-hub). Our year-end target is 25 stores; the first two have not opened yet.
