# Mac OS X reaches customers

[Mac OS X](context-use://entity/mac-os-x) began shipping on March 24, 2001, the date [Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs)’s team had [announced in January](context-use://page/a-firm-date-for-mac-os-x).

The shipping date is corroborated retrospectively by Apple’s [May preinstallation record](context-use://record/osx-preinstall). That later source supplies evidence of a March event; its publication date does not become the event date. The earlier [summer 2000 expectation](context-use://page/aqua-makes-the-next-mac-visible) remains part of the story.
