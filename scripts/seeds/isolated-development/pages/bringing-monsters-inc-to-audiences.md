# Bringing Monsters, Inc. to audiences

Our [Pixar](context-use://entity/pixar) film [Monsters, Inc.](context-use://entity/monsters-inc) opened in the US through [Disney](context-use://entity/disney) on November 2, 2001. [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) lead the studio as chairman and CEO; [Pete Docter](context-use://entity/pete-docter) directed the film. The [release and credits](context-use://record/monsters-release) record the team’s work.

The [production account](context-use://record/monsters-craft) connects the industrial monster world with the characters and story. Our technical work serves the relationship between Sulley and Boo. The [Disney film page](https://movies.disney.com/monsters-inc) has the trailer and production videos.
