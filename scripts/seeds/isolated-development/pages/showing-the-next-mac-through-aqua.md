# Showing the next Mac through Aqua

At Macworld San Francisco on January 5, 2000, [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) demonstrated [Mac OS X](context-use://entity/mac-os-x). Aqua and the Dock make our new UNIX-based foundation visible to Mac users.

Our [announcement](context-use://record/osx-preview) targets commercial availability in summer 2000. We still have to deliver that system and support customers who depend on existing Mac applications.
