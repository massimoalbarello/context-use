# Putting the Cube on ice

On July 3, 2001, we suspended [Power Mac G4 Cube](context-use://entity/power-mac-g4-cube) production indefinitely at [Apple](context-use://entity/apple). [Phil Schiller](context-use://entity/phil-schiller) explained that most customers had chosen the G4 minitower instead.

[My](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) [Cube bet](context-use://page/my-bet-on-the-cube) has reached a production halt, even after the [CD-burning update](context-use://page/giving-the-cube-a-music-workflow). Our [announcement](context-use://record/cube-ice) leaves a small possibility of a future model, but we have no plan for one.
