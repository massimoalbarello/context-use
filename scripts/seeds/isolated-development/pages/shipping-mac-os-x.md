# Shipping Mac OS X

Our [Mac OS X](context-use://entity/mac-os-x) release began shipping on March 24, 2001, meeting the date [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) had [announced in January](context-use://page/committing-to-march-24-for-mac-os-x).

We have moved from the [summer 2000 target](context-use://page/showing-the-next-mac-through-aqua) to a shipping system. Apple’s [later preinstallation announcement](context-use://record/osx-preinstall) also confirms the March 24 release date. Getting the system into customers’ hands is a milestone in our transition, not its completion.
