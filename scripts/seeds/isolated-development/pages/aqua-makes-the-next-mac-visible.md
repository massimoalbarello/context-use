# Aqua makes the next Mac visible

[Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) demonstrated [Mac OS X](context-use://entity/mac-os-x) at Macworld San Francisco: Aqua and the Dock made a new UNIX-based foundation visible to ordinary Mac users.

The [announcement](context-use://record/osx-preview) promised summer 2000 commercial availability. That is the expectation at this point, not a statement that the software already shipped. For Steve, the challenge is to renew the Mac while existing customers still depend on their applications.
