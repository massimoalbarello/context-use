# From a home movie to a DVD

On February 19, [Apple](context-use://entity/apple) began shipping SuperDrive-equipped Power Mac G4 systems with [iMovie](context-use://entity/imovie), [iDVD](context-use://entity/idvd) and [iTunes](context-use://entity/itunes). The [shipping record](seed-record:superdrive) ties software to hardware that can write the finished disc.

For [Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs), the significance is the complete customer task: bring a movie into the Mac, edit it, and produce something playable away from the computer. It extends the [digital-hub proposition](context-use://page/the-mac-as-a-digital-hub).
