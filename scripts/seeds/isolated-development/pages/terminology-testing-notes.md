# Terminology testing notes

[Priya Shah](context-use://entity/priya-shah) and [Maya Chen](context-use://entity/maya-chen) tested labels for the [Northstar](context-use://entity/northstar) map with people who had never seen the workspace.

## Result

“Knowledge page” was clearer than “page cloud.” “Hypermedia” worked when paired with a map icon and an immediately explorable canvas. Carry the vocabulary into the [design critique notes](context-use://page/design-critique-notes).
