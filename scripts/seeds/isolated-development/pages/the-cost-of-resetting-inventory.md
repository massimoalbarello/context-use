# The cost of resetting inventory

The quarter ending December 30, 2000 brought a $195 million net loss on $1 billion revenue. [Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) reported about five and a half weeks of channel inventory; [Fred Anderson](context-use://entity/fred-anderson) emphasized cash and the intention to regain profitability.

The [results record](seed-record:q1-loss) was published January 17, 2001. Its fiscal-year label does not move the underlying quarter into calendar 2001. This is a setback inside Apple’s renewal, not a straight-line success story.
