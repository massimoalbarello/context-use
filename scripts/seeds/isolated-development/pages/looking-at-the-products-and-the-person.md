# Looking at the products and the person

The [Cube photograph](context-use://asset/power-mac-g4-cube-photo) shows the [Power Mac G4 Cube](context-use://entity/power-mac-g4-cube) with its display and peripherals; it was taken in 2005. The [original iPod image](context-use://asset/original-ipod-photo) illustrates the [first-generation player](context-use://entity/ipod), but the derivative was uploaded in 2006 and its capture date is unconfirmed.

The [Steve Jobs portrait](context-use://asset/steve-jobs-portrait-2010) is from June 2010 and is used only to identify [Steve](context-use://entity/steve-jobs). None is presented as a photograph of a 2000–2001 meeting or launch. [Credits and licenses](context-use://asset/historical-asset-credits) accompany all three files.
