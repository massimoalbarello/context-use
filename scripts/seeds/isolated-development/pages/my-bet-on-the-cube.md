# My bet on the Cube

As of July 19, 2000, [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) have [introduced the Cube](context-use://page/introducing-the-cube). The [Power Mac G4 Cube](context-use://entity/power-mac-g4-cube) gives our desktop lineup a compact, distinctive alternative alongside the towers.

The commercial question is still open: will enough customers choose this form? The launch establishes what we are offering, while sales will establish demand.
