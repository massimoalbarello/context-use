# Monsters Inc reaches an audience

[Monsters, Inc.](context-use://entity/monsters-inc) opened in the US through [Disney](context-use://entity/disney) on November 2, while [Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) led [Pixar](context-use://entity/pixar) as well as Apple. [Disney’s release reference](seed-record:monsters-release) fixes the date.

[Pete Docter](context-use://entity/pete-docter) directed the film. Jobs’s studio leadership is a different responsibility from direction or screenplay authorship. [Pixar’s production account](seed-record:monsters-craft) connects the industrial monster world to the characters and story. The official [Disney film page](https://movies.disney.com/monsters-inc) also offers a trailer and production videos.
