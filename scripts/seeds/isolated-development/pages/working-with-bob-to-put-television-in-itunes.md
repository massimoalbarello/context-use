# Working with Bob to put television in iTunes

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) am working with [Bob Iger](context-use://entity/bob-iger) and [Disney](context-use://entity/disney) to bring television into our [store](context-use://entity/itunes-store). Our [iTunes 6 release](context-use://record/itunes-video) offers US episodes of Lost and Desperate Housewives for $1.99, as well as music videos and [Pixar](context-use://entity/pixar) shorts.

Customers can move those downloads onto [our video-capable iPod](context-use://page/giving-our-ipod-a-video-screen). The content agreement and the hardware launch have to work together.
