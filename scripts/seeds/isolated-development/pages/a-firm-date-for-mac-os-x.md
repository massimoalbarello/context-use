# A firm date for Mac OS X

On January 9, [Apple](context-use://entity/apple) announced March 24 availability for [Mac OS X](context-use://entity/mac-os-x) at $129. Its [dated record](context-use://record/osx-date) reports more than 100,000 beta copies and 75,000 feedback entries.

For [Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs), this turns the [public beta](context-use://page/listening-through-a-public-beta) into a public delivery commitment. Those totals show participation, not a guarantee that every compatibility problem is solved.
