# Our financial position before iPod

On October 17, 2001, we reported $66 million in quarterly net profit at [Apple](context-use://entity/apple) for the period ended September 29. In our [results announcement](context-use://record/q4-profit), [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) pointed to progress in software, education and retail.

Together with our [earlier return to profit](context-use://page/returning-to-quarterly-profit), the [quarterly results table](context-use://asset/apple-quarterly-results) tracks our recovery. This quarter ended before our iPod announcement, so its results measure the business we already had.
