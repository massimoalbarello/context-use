# My work — 2000 to 2001

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) am leading [Apple and Pixar](context-use://page/my-two-ceo-roles) through two busy years. At Apple, we are moving the Mac to a new software foundation and extending what people can do with it. At Pixar, we have a new home and a new film reaching audiences.

[My collaborators](context-use://page/my-collaborators-at-apple), [my responsibilities at both companies](context-use://page/my-work-at-apple-and-pixar) and [my working notes](context-use://page/my-working-notes-and-source-material) connect the work. Our [digital-hub model](context-use://page/our-digital-hub-model), [store experience](context-use://page/helping-customers-in-our-stores) and [products and their uses](context-use://page/the-products-we-make-and-how-people-use-them) capture the relationships across projects.

## Rebuilding the Mac

We have taken [OS X](context-use://page/moving-the-mac-to-os-x) from preview through beta to shipping and 10.1. Our [digital-hub work](context-use://page/the-mac-as-our-digital-hub) now includes music, movies and a portable player built by [the iPod team](context-use://page/the-team-behind-our-first-ipod). [My Cube bet](context-use://page/my-bet-on-the-cube) has gone from launch to a production halt.

## Reaching customers

[Ron and I](context-use://page/building-our-retail-channel-with-ron) have opened our first stores around demonstrations and help. Our [financial checkpoint before iPod](context-use://page/our-financial-position-before-ipod) records the recovery already under way. [Photos of me and our products](context-use://page/photos-of-me-and-our-products) provide visual references.

## My work at Pixar

We have brought [Monsters, Inc. to audiences](context-use://page/bringing-monsters-inc-to-audiences). I lead the studio; Pete Docter and the filmmaking team carry the creative work on the film.
