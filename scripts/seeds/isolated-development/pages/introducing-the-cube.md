# Introducing the Cube

On July 19, 2000, [I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) introduced the [Power Mac G4 Cube](context-use://entity/power-mac-g4-cube) alongside new iMacs and dual-processor Power Macs. It adds a compact desktop in a clear enclosure to our Mac family.

Our [launch announcement](context-use://record/cube-launch) describes a 450 MHz G4 and a DVD drive. [iMovie](context-use://entity/imovie) 2 connects the new lineup to making home movies. We have a distinctive new machine; sustained customer demand is still to be established.
