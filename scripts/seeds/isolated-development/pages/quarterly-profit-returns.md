# Quarterly profit returns

On April 18, [Apple](context-use://entity/apple) reported $43 million net profit for the quarter ended March 31, following the [inventory-reset loss](context-use://page/the-cost-of-resetting-inventory). Revenue was $1.43 billion, still lower than a year earlier.

The [results](context-use://record/q2-profit) give [Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) and [Fred Anderson](context-use://entity/fred-anderson) an improving quarterly picture, with $40 million profit excluding the stated non-recurring items. Recovery and growth are separate claims.
