# The boundaries I work across

[I](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) need partners where a device alone cannot complete the experience. [Music rights](context-use://page/selling-songs-one-at-a-time) and [EMI’s different purchase option](context-use://page/opening-emi-purchases-beyond-drm) govern what customers can buy and play. [Disney television](context-use://page/working-with-bob-to-put-television-in-itunes) brings another kind of content.

[Motorola’s handset](context-use://page/shipping-itunes-on-the-rokr) and our carrier, [AT&T](context-use://entity/at-t), involve different responsibilities from making [our own phone](context-use://page/bringing-our-music-work-into-phones). [The Mac’s Intel transition](context-use://page/changing-the-processor-underneath-our-mac) is another platform commitment to carry alongside them.
