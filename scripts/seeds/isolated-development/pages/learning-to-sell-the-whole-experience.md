# Learning to sell the whole experience

As of May 15, 2001, [Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs)’s [retail plan](context-use://page/the-first-stores-are-announced) is public. The first two [Apple Retail Stores](context-use://entity/apple-retail-stores) have not yet opened.

Their proposed demonstrations bring hardware, software and advice together. The commercial question is still open: will that experience persuade visitors to buy?
