# A music library in the hand

On October 23, [Steve Jobs](context-use://entity/steve-jobs) announced [iPod](context-use://entity/ipod), linking a pocket player to the [iTunes music library](context-use://page/music-becomes-a-mac-workflow). Its [launch record](context-use://record/ipod-launch) describes FireWire Auto-Sync, 5 GB storage and $399 US pricing.

November 10 is the announced availability date, not the keynote date. The one-handed scroll wheel makes a large library usable; synchronization keeps its organization tied to the Mac.
