# Historical anchors

These sources establish the public facts used by the fixtures. The private first-person
messages and internal-review connective tissue in `stories/` are fictionalized.

## Apple and Microsoft

- [Microsoft and Apple affirm commitment to build next-generation Macintosh software](https://news.microsoft.com/source/1997/08/06/microsoft-and-apple-affirm-commitment-to-build-next-generation-software-for-macintosh/)
  — Office and Internet Explorer support, default-browser agreement, patent cross-license,
  Java collaboration, and Microsoft's $150 million non-voting investment.

## iMac

- [Smithsonian: iMac G3 personal computer](https://www.si.edu/object/imac-g3-personal-computer%3Achndm_2000-63-1-a_c)
  — translucent blue enclosure, integrated form, carrying handle, and Apple Industrial
  Design Team attribution.
- [Macworld: the original iMac](https://www.macworld.com/article/190458/original_imac.html)
  — 6 May 1998 introduction and contemporary launch context.

## iPod and iTunes

- [Apple presents iPod](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2001/10/23Apple-Presents-iPod/)
  — 1,000 songs, 5 GB, FireWire, battery life, Auto-Sync, price, and availability.
- [Apple launches the iTunes Music Store](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2003/04/28Apple-Launches-the-iTunes-Music-Store/)
  — label participants, 99-cent pricing, usage rights, initial catalog, and iTunes 4.

## Mobile partnerships and iPhone

- [Apple, Motorola and Cingular launch the first phone with iTunes](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2005/09/07Apple-Motorola-Cingular-Launch-Worlds-First-Mobile-Phone-with-iTunes/)
  — participants, product capacity, exclusive distribution, and pricing.
- [Apple reinvents the phone with iPhone](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2007/01/09Apple-Reinvents-the-Phone-with-iPhone/)
  — product positioning, multi-touch interface, exclusive US carrier, capacities, prices,
  and June availability.
- [iPhone premieres at Apple retail stores](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2007/06/28iPhone-Premieres-This-Friday-Night-at-Apple-Retail-Stores/)
  — 29 June 2007 on-sale time and Apple/AT&T retail distribution.
